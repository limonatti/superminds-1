🎧 Аудирование

A new student

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно.

Teacher: Good morning! Are you a new student?
Sofia: Yes, I am. My name's Sofia.
Teacher: Nice to meet you, Sofia. What's your surname?
Sofia: It's Marin. M-A-R-I-N.
Teacher: Thank you. Where are you from?
Sofia: I'm from Argentina, but I live here now.
Teacher: How old are you?
Sofia: I'm thirteen.
Teacher: Have you got a phone number?
Sofia: Yes. It's oh-two-one, four-five-six-seven.
Teacher: Great. Have you got a pencil case and a calculator?
Sofia: I've got a pencil case, but I haven't got a calculator.
Teacher: No problem. Here's one for you. Welcome to our school!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (в классе)

In the classroom

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Sofia: Excuse me, Mr Brown. Can I ask a question?
Mr Brown: Of course. Go ahead.
Sofia: What's this in English?
Mr Brown: That's an eraser. You use it with a pencil.
Sofia: And can I borrow an exercise book, please?
Mr Brown: Yes, sure. Here you are.
Sofia: Thank you. Can I sit next to Mia?
Mr Brown: Yes, you can. But please listen carefully today.
Sofia: OK, I will. Thank you, Mr Brown.

📕 Чтение

My new friend

This is my new friend. Her name's Lena and she's from Turkey. She's fourteen years old. Lena has got long, dark hair and brown eyes.

She can speak three languages: Turkish, English and a little Russian. She can play the guitar and she can dance really well, but she can't ride a bike. Lena is friendly and funny, and I'm happy she's in my class.

📕 Чтение 2 (A profile)

About me

Hello! I'm Daniel and I'm from Mexico. I'm twelve years old. I've got one brother and two sisters.

I can play football and I can sing, but I can't swim. At school my favourite things are my calculator and my new pen. I haven't got a pet, but I want a dog! Please write to me and tell me about you.

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

All about me. Напиши короткий профиль о себе (5–6 предложений): имя, возраст, страна, что у тебя есть (I've got…) и что ты умеешь/не умеешь (I can… / I can't…).

Проверь: краткие формы (I'm, I've got), a / an перед словом, заглавные буквы у имён и стран.

Welcome to Greenwood School! We are happy to have new students from many countries. In our school you can study English, maths, science and art.
