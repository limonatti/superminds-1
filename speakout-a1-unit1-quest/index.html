Ключи и проверка открытых ответов

1: Hello → Hello, Good morning, Good evening; Goodbye → See you, Good night, Bye. 2: Mia / Italy / a student. 3: Italy–Italian, Japan–Japanese, the UK–British, Brazil–Brazilian. 4: a doctor / a taxi driver / a singer / a waiter. 5: am / Are / is / isn't / is / an. 6: What is your name? / Where are you from? / How do you spell your surname? 7: Mia / Stone / 5930862. 8: a phone, two keys, an umbrella. 9: bag A; пример ответа “Hi, Mia. I have your bag.” 10: свободные ответы; проверьте be, a/an, have/has, spelling и понятность сообщения.
