🎧 Аудирование

At the shopping centre

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Ella: Look at this dress! Do you like it?
Sam: Yes, it's nice. But you're wearing a lot of black today.
Ella: I know. I want something with more colour. What about this red skirt?
Sam: It suits you. Are you going to try it on?
Ella: Yes. What size is it?
Sam: It says medium. Here you are.
Ella: Thanks. Oh, and I need new trainers for the party.
Sam: The party? Everyone is wearing smart clothes, not trainers!
Ella: Good point. Then I'm buying these shoes instead.

🎧 Аудирование 2

Making arrangements

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Sam: Hi, Ella. Are you free on Saturday?
Ella: I think so. Why?
Sam: There's a new clothes shop in town. I'm meeting Ben there at eleven.
Ella: Sounds good. Where exactly?
Sam: Let's meet in front of the cinema, then we can walk together.
Ella: OK. What are you wearing so I can find you?
Sam: My grey jumper and blue jeans. See you Saturday!

📕 Чтение

Teenage pressures

Many teenagers feel they have to wear the 'right' clothes. If everyone at school is wearing a certain brand, some students feel unhappy or left out without it.

But fashion is expensive, and the newest trainers or the coolest jacket can cost a lot of money. Some teenagers spend all their pocket money on clothes; others feel stressed because their families can't buy expensive brands.

Experts say that real style is about confidence, not price. You don't have to follow every trend. Wearing clothes that are comfortable and that you like is more important than wearing the most popular label.

📕 Чтение 2

My favourite clothes

Hi! I'm Nadia and I love fashion, but I don't follow expensive brands. Right now I'm wearing my favourite outfit: black leggings, a long grey jumper and white trainers.

I usually wear casual clothes at the weekend, but today is special because I'm meeting my friends in town. In winter I always wear a warm coat and a colourful scarf. I never wear ties or smart shoes — they aren't comfortable for me!

My advice? Choose clothes that make you feel good. Today I feel great, and that's real style.

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

An email. Напиши другу неформальное письмо (5–6 предложений): пригласи в новый магазин одежды, напиши когда и где встретиться, что ты сейчас делаешь и что наденешь.

Проверь: Present continuous (I'm wearing / we're meeting), связки (and, but, because), начало и конец письма (Hi…, See you soon!).

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Fast fashion and the planet

'Fast fashion' means cheap clothes that are made very quickly and sold in huge amounts. Shops offer new styles every few weeks, so people buy more and more clothes.

But fast fashion is a big problem for the planet. Making clothes uses a lot of water and energy, and many people throw old clothes away after wearing them only a few times. These clothes often end up in huge piles of rubbish.

Today, more teenagers are choosing a different way. Some buy second-hand clothes, others swap clothes with friends or repair the clothes they already have. They believe that looking good and helping the planet can go together.
