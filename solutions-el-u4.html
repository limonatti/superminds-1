🎧 Аудирование

In the kitchen

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Lily: Mum, is there any cheese in the fridge?
Mum: Yes, there's some on the top shelf. Why?
Lily: I'm making a sandwich. Are there any tomatoes?
Mum: No, there aren't any. But there are some cucumbers.
Lily: OK. How much bread is there?
Mum: There's a lot of bread. And there are a few apples too, if you want fruit.
Lily: Great. Can I have a glass of orange juice as well?
Mum: Of course. There isn't much juice left, so finish it.
Lily: Thanks, Mum! This sandwich is delicious.

🎧 Аудирование 2

At a restaurant

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Waiter: Good evening. Are you ready to order?
Guest: Yes. I'd like the chicken with rice, please.
Waiter: Would you like any vegetables with that?
Guest: Yes, some peas and carrots, please.
Waiter: And to drink?
Guest: Can I have a glass of water? How much is the fruit salad?
Waiter: It's four pounds fifty. Would you like one?
Guest: Yes, please. Thank you.

📕 Чтение

Unusual restaurants

Around the world, there are some very unusual restaurants. In one restaurant in Japan, robots cook the food and bring it to your table. There aren't any human waiters at all!

In another restaurant, you eat in complete darkness. There isn't any light, so you can't see your food. The idea is that your other senses become stronger, and the food tastes even better.

There is also a restaurant under the sea. You sit at your table and watch fish swimming around you through big glass windows. These places are expensive, but people love them because they are so different.

📕 Чтение 2

A freegan's day

Marco is a 'freegan'. This means he doesn't buy food from shops. Instead, he collects food that supermarkets throw away.

'Shops throw away a lot of food that is still good,' says Marco. 'There are always some vegetables and a bit of bread at the end of the day. I don't take much — just enough for my dinner.' Last night he made soup with carrots, potatoes and onions.

Marco says that people waste too much food. 'There isn't any need to throw so much away. If we are careful, there is enough food for everyone.'

✍️ Writing

An invitation. Напиши приглашение другу на ужин (5–6 предложений): что ты готовишь, какие продукты у тебя есть, когда и во сколько прийти, и попроси что-то принести.

Проверь: there is / there are, some / any, would you like…?, вежливые формы.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Eat the rainbow

Doctors often say we should 'eat the rainbow'. This means eating fruit and vegetables of many different colours, because each colour gives your body something different.

Red foods like tomatoes and strawberries are good for your heart. Orange foods, such as carrots, help your eyes. Green vegetables like lettuce and peas have a lot of vitamins and give you energy. Even purple foods, like some grapes, are healthy.

Experts say that teenagers should eat at least five portions of fruit and vegetables every day. It doesn't have to be difficult: an apple, a few carrots or a glass of fresh juice all count. A colourful plate is usually a healthy plate.
