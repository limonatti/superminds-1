Planning a trip

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Ella: Jack, have you ever been to Italy?
Jack: No, I've never been there. But I'm going to visit Rome this summer!
Ella: Lucky you! How are you going to travel?
Jack: By plane. I'm going to book the tickets tonight.
Ella: Have you booked a hotel yet?
Jack: Yes, a small one near the centre. I've already packed my guidebook!
Ella: Have you ever tried real Italian pizza?
Jack: Never, but I'm definitely going to eat a lot of it!

📕 Чтение

A different holiday

Most tourists go to hotels near the beach, but some people like different holidays. Every summer, Marta and her family go camping in the mountains. They have never stayed in a hotel on holiday.

This year they are going to try something new: a long train journey across the country. "We're going to visit six cities," says Marta. "I've never been so excited!"
