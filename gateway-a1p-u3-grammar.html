📖 Разбор грамматики

Present simple — affirmative
I / you / we / they + глагол: I play football.
he / she / it + глагол-s: She plays tennis.
-es после -o,-ch,-sh,-ss: goes, watches. -y→-ies: study→studies.
Present simple — negative
I/you/we/they + don't + глагол: I don't like it.
he/she/it + doesn't + глагол: He doesn't play.
После doesn't глагол БЕЗ -s: She doesn't watch TV.
Questions — yes/no & wh-
Do you…? / Does he…? — Yes, I do. / No, he doesn't.
wh-: What do you do? · Where does she live? · What time do you get up?
Adverbs of frequency — как часто
always (100%) · usually · often · sometimes · never (0%)
Перед смысловым глаголом: I always read. После to be: She is often late.
