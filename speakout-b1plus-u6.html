A new city

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Dan: You moved to Lisbon three months ago, right? How is it?
Mia: I love it! But it took time. At first the city was so noisy that I couldn't sleep.
Dan: And now?
Mia: Now I'm used to the noise. I've even got used to the hills — there are such steep streets here!
Dan: What about transport?
Mia: I take the old yellow tram. It's such a beautiful ride that tourists queue for it every morning.
Dan: Do you miss anything from home?
Mia: Snow! I used to go skiing every winter. But honestly — the ocean is a good exchange.

📕 Чтение

The city of tomorrow

What will cities look like in fifty years? Architects imagine tall towers covered in plants, roofs full of gardens, and streets with cycle lanes instead of cars. In the city of tomorrow, you might grow vegetables on your balcony and get around by electric bus or bike.

Not everyone will love it. Some people enjoy the hustle and bustle of a busy centre; others dream of quiet green spaces. The challenge is to build cities that are clean and modern but still feel human. One thing is certain: the people who move there will need time to get used to a very different way of life.

🗺️ How to… посоветовать, что посмотреть

You should definitely visit the old town. — обязательно сходи
It's worth seeing the castle at sunset. — стоит увидеть
I wouldn't go there on a Saturday — it's too crowded.
How do I get to the station? — как добраться?
