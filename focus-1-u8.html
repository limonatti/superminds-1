🎧 Аудирование

A day trip

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Nina: Sam, what's the weather like tomorrow? Let's go for a walk.
Sam: The app says it will be sunny in the morning.
Nina: Great! If it's sunny, we'll go to the lake.
Sam: Good idea. But it might be windy in the afternoon.
Nina: That's OK. If it gets windy, we'll come home early.
Sam: Do you think we'll see any animals?
Nina: Maybe some birds and fish. I hope we won't see any snakes!
Sam: Don't worry. Let's take a map and some water.

📕 Чтение

Our changing planet

Our planet is beautiful, with high mountains, big forests and deep oceans. But nature is in danger. Scientists say that if we don't protect it, many animals will disappear.

The good news is that everyone can help. If we use less plastic and plant more trees, the world will be a better place. Small actions today will make a big difference tomorrow.
