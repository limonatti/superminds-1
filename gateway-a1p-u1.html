🎧 Аудирование

Nice to meet you

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Sofia: Hi! I'm Sofia. What's your name?
Deniz: Hello, Sofia. My name's Deniz. Nice to meet you.
Sofia: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from, Deniz?
Deniz: I'm from Istanbul, so I'm Turkish. And you?
Sofia: I'm from Madrid. I'm Spanish. Are you a new student here?
Deniz: Yes, I am. I'm thirteen. How old are you?
Sofia: I'm thirteen too! Have you got any brothers or sisters?
Deniz: I've got one sister. Her name is Elif. She isn't here — she's in Turkey with my grandparents.
Sofia: Cool! This is my brother, Mateo. He's in my class.
Deniz: Hi, Mateo. Nice to meet you both!

🎧 Аудирование 2

My family

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Deniz: Sofia, is this a photo of your family?
Sofia: Yes! This is my mother. Her name's Lucía, and my father's name is Pablo.
Deniz: And who are they?
Sofia: They're my grandparents. My grandmother is from Brazil, so I'm half Brazilian!
Deniz: Really? Have you got any cousins?
Sofia: Yes, I've got three cousins. My aunt and uncle live in Rio.
Deniz: Your family is very international!
Sofia: I know. We speak Spanish and Portuguese at home.

📕 Чтение

Faces of our school

Our school is small, but the students come from all over the world. Today we say hello to three of them.

Amir is fourteen and he is Egyptian. His family is from Cairo, but now they live in Manchester in the UK. He has got two brothers and a big, noisy family. His favourite subject is science.

Mei is thirteen and she is Chinese. She is an only child, so she hasn't got any brothers or sisters. She lives with her mother, her father and her grandmother. Her grandmother tells her wonderful stories about China.

Lucas is from São Paulo, so he is Brazilian. He has got one sister and lots of cousins. 'My family is very big and very loud,' he says. 'When we are together, we are never quiet!'

📕 Чтение 2

A country of many faces

The UK is a small country, but the people in it come from everywhere. In big cities like London, you can hear more than 100 languages on the streets.

About one in eight people in the UK was born in another country. Their families are from India, Poland, Nigeria, Turkey and many other places. Together, they make the country colourful and interesting.

Zara is a good example. Her mother is British and her father is Turkish. 'I've got two passports and two families,' she says. 'Half of my family is in London and half is in Ankara. I think that's lucky!'

✍️ Writing

A personal profile. Напиши короткий профиль о себе (6–7 предложений): имя, возраст, страна и национальность, семья (have got), любимый предмет.

Проверь: to be (am/is/are), have got, притяжательные местоимения (my, his, her), заглавные буквы у стран и национальностей.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

The UK in numbers

Numbers can tell us a lot about a country. Here are some facts about the people who live in the United Kingdom today.

The average family in the UK has got 2.4 people. About one in six people is over 65 years old, and there are 3.5 million children under five. The average age of the population is around 40.

People in the UK come from all over the world. About one in eight people was born in another country, and together they speak more than 300 languages. Numbers like these show that a modern country is a mix of many families, many nationalities and many stories.
