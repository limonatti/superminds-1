🎧 Аудирование

The big match

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Ben: Did you watch the basketball final last night, Zoe?
Zoe: Yes, I did! It was amazing. Who won?
Ben: The Eagles won. The score was 88 to 85. It was very close!
Zoe: Was Marco Silva in the team?
Ben: Yes, he was. He was the best player. He scored 30 points!
Zoe: Wow! Were there a lot of people at the match?
Ben: Yes, there were. The stadium was full. It was a great atmosphere.
Zoe: I'm sad I missed it. When is the next match?
Ben: Next Saturday. Let's watch it together!

🎧 Аудирование 2

A famous champion

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Lily: We're doing a project about a famous champion. Who did you choose, Sam?
Sam: I chose a swimmer. She won five gold medals at the Olympics.
Lily: Amazing! When did she start swimming?
Sam: She started when she was six. She trained every day for years.
Lily: Was it difficult for her?
Sam: Yes, it was. But she never gave up. She said, 'I wanted to be the best.'
Lily: That's a great story. Did she win any other prizes?
Sam: Yes, she got lots of cups and medals. She was a real champion!

📕 Чтение

The first modern Olympics

Today the Olympic Games are huge, but the first modern Olympics in 1896 were very different.

The Games were in Athens, in Greece. There were only about 240 athletes, and they were all men — there weren't any women! There were nine sports, including swimming, cycling and gymnastics. The winners didn't get gold medals; they got silver medals and a branch from an olive tree.

One famous moment was the marathon. A Greek runner called Spyridon Louis won the race, and the whole country was very happy. It was the start of a great tradition that continues today.

📕 Чтение 2

An unusual team sport

When you think of team sports, you probably think of football or basketball. But have you heard of 'sepak takraw'?

This sport is very popular in South-East Asia. It is like volleyball, but there is one big difference: you can't use your hands! Players use their feet, knees and head to hit the ball over the net. The ball is small and made of plastic.

Sepak takraw is very exciting to watch because the players jump high and do amazing kicks. Last year, there was a big competition in Thailand, and thousands of people came to watch. Maybe one day it will be an Olympic sport!

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

The girl who changed football

A hundred years ago, football was only a sport for men. But one team changed people's minds.

In England, during the First World War, many men were away, so women started playing football in factories. One team, the Dick, Kerr Ladies, became very famous. In 1920, there was a match with 53,000 people watching! The players were strong and skilful, and the crowds loved them.

Sadly, in 1921 the football association stopped women playing on their pitches, and women's football almost disappeared for fifty years. But today, women's football is popular again, and those early players are finally getting the respect they deserved.
