🎧 Аудирование

Which one is your sister?

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Dan: Kate, is your sister here? What does she look like?
Kate: Yes, she's over there. She's taller than me and she's got long dark hair.
Dan: Is she the girl with glasses?
Kate: No, that's her friend. My sister is wearing a red dress.
Dan: Oh, I see her! She looks really friendly.
Kate: She is. She's the kindest person I know — and much funnier than me!
Dan: Are you the same age?
Kate: No, she's older. She's nineteen and I'm sixteen.

📕 Чтение

Twins but different

Anna and Ela are twins, but they are very different. Anna is taller and has short fair hair. She is quiet and shy, and she loves reading.

Ela has long dark hair. She is louder and much more sporty than her sister. People say Ela is the funniest girl in their class. The girls look different, but they are best friends.

💬 How to… описать человека

What does he/she look like? — как выглядит
He's tall and has got dark hair. — внешность
She's really friendly / a bit shy. — характер
