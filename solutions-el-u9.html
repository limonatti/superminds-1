🎧 Аудирование

University accommodation

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Mum: Welcome to your new room! Have you unpacked yet?
Leo: Almost. I've already put my clothes in the wardrobe.
Mum: Great. Have you made the bed?
Leo: Yes, I've just made it. But I haven't put up the shelves yet.
Mum: No problem, we'll do that later. What do you think of the room?
Leo: I love it! It's bigger than my old room, and there's a big desk for studying.
Mum: And a comfortable sofa. Have you found the light switch?
Leo: Yes, it's behind the curtains. Thanks, Mum. It feels like home already!

🎧 Аудирование 2

Comparing two homes

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Ben: Look at these two flats. Which one do you prefer?
Ella: The first one looks brighter. It has big windows and a modern kitchen.
Ben: True. But the second one has a bigger living room and a nice sofa.
Ella: Yes, but it looks a bit dark. And there's no washing machine.
Ben: Good point. I think the first flat is more comfortable.
Ella: I agree. Let's choose the first one.

📕 Чтение

In the middle of nowhere

Some people choose to live far away from cities, in the middle of nowhere. The Müller family lives in a small wooden house on an island, three hours by boat from the nearest town.

'We have everything we need,' says Mrs Müller. 'We've built our own house, we grow our own food, and we get electricity from the sun. Life is simple but happy.' Their children have never lived in a city and they love the freedom of the island.

Of course, there are difficulties. When someone is ill, the journey to a hospital is long. But the family says they have never regretted their choice. 'Our home is quiet, beautiful and completely ours.'

📕 Чтение 2

My dream home

I've lived in the same flat all my life, and I love it, but I've always dreamed of a different kind of home. My dream house is a big cottage in the countryside, with a garden full of flowers.

Inside, there would be a huge kitchen where my whole family could cook together, a cosy living room with a fireplace, and a big bedroom with a view of the hills. I've already chosen the colours in my imagination!

I haven't got this home yet, of course. But I've started to save money, and one day I hope my dream will come true. What does your dream home look like?

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

A description. Опиши свой дом или квартиру (5–6 предложений): какие есть комнаты, какая мебель, что тебе нравится, и что бы ты хотел изменить.

Проверь: названия мебели и комнат, предлоги места (in, on, next to), прилагательные (comfortable, modern, cosy).

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Homes of the future

What will our homes look like in fifty years? Experts have already started to design the 'smart homes' of the future, and some of their ideas are amazing.

In a smart home, you can control the lights, the heating and even the cooker with your voice or your phone. Some houses have already been built with walls that produce electricity from the sun, so they don't need energy from outside. Robots help with the housework, and fridges order food when it runs out.

But not everyone wants a high-tech home. Many people say that a real home is not about technology. It is about the people you love and the memories you make there. Perhaps the best home of the future will mix clever technology with a warm, human heart.
