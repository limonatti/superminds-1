🎧 Аудирование

Nice to meet you

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Marta: Hi! I'm Marta. What's your name?
Alex: I'm Alex. Nice to meet you. Where are you from, Marta?
Marta: I'm from Poland, but now I live here. Are you Italian?
Alex: No, I'm not. I'm Spanish. Have you got a big family?
Marta: Yes, I have. I've got two brothers and a sister.
Alex: Cool! Is this your new flat?
Marta: Yes. There are three rooms. This is the living room.
Alex: It's nice! There's a big sofa and a lovely armchair.

📕 Чтение

My home

Hi! I'm Nadia and I'm Russian. I live in a small flat with my family. We haven't got a garden, but the flat is nice and warm.

There are four rooms: a kitchen, a bathroom, a living room and one bedroom. In my favourite room, the living room, there is a big sofa, two armchairs and a lamp next to the window. This is my home and I love it!

🏠 Домашнее задание

Обо мне и моём доме. Напиши 6–7 предложений: как тебя зовут, откуда ты и какая у тебя национальность, кто есть в семье (have got), и опиши одну комнату (there is / there are + мебель).
