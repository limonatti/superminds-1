🎧 Аудирование

A strange night

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Amy: You look tired, Tom. Did you sleep badly?
Tom: Terribly. Something strange happened last night.
Amy: Ooh, tell me! What happened?
Tom: Well, I was reading in bed when I heard a noise downstairs.
Amy: Scary! Had you locked the door?
Tom: Yes, I had. So I went down very quietly... and it was just the cat!
Amy: Ha! Your cat has done that before, hasn't it?
Tom: Twice this week! I've never been so frightened by a cat in my life.

📕 Чтение

Why we love scary stories

Humans have told stories for thousands of years. Long before books or films, people sat around a fire and shared tales of heroes, monsters and strange events. A good story has a clear plot, interesting characters and, very often, a surprising twist at the end.

Scary stories are especially popular. Why do we enjoy being frightened? Scientists say that when we feel safe — on a sofa, not in real danger — a little fear is exciting. Our heart beats faster and then we relax. It turned out that sharing a scary story with friends also brings people closer together. So next time the lights go out, tell a tale!

🏠 Домашнее задание

Расскажи историю! Напиши короткую историю (5–6 предложений) о странном или смешном случае. Используй narrative tenses: Past Simple для событий, Past Continuous для фона (While I was…), Past Perfect для того, что случилось раньше. Начни так: «It was a normal evening. I was …ing when suddenly …»
