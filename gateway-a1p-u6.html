🎧 Аудирование

At the clothes shop

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Assistant: Good morning. Can I help you?
Tom: Yes, please. I'm looking for a jumper.
Assistant: What size are you?
Tom: Medium, I think. Have you got this one in blue?
Assistant: Let me see. Yes, here you are. It's on sale — it's only fifteen pounds.
Tom: Great! Can I try it on?
Assistant: Of course. The changing room is over there.
Tom: It fits perfectly. I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
Assistant: Yes, of course. Thank you. Here's your receipt.

🎧 Аудирование 2

What are you doing?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Mia: Hi Leo, where are you? What are you doing?
Leo: I'm at the shopping centre. I'm buying a birthday present for my sister.
Mia: Oh nice! What are you looking at?
Leo: I'm looking at some boots in the shoe shop. But they're expensive.
Mia: Is your sister wearing boots these days?
Leo: Yes, she loves them. Wait — a shop assistant is coming. I'll call you back!
Mia: OK. Are you having lunch there?
Leo: Yes, I'm meeting Dad at the café at one. See you later!

🔤 Word Skills (работа со словом)

Present continuous: am/is/are + глагол-ing (сейчас).

📕 Чтение

Shopping without money?

In some towns, there are special shops where you don't need any money. They are called 'swap shops'.

In a swap shop, people bring clothes and things they don't want any more. You leave your old jacket or a book, and you take something else — for free! It is a fun way to get 'new' clothes without spending cash.

'I love our swap shop,' says Ella, who is wearing a jumper from there today. 'It's good for my purse and good for the planet, because we don't buy so many new things.' More and more towns are opening swap shops now.

📕 Чтение 2

The world's biggest shopping centre

Do you like shopping? Then you will love the biggest shopping centres in the world. Some of them are like small cities!

In these enormous places there are hundreds of shops, but also cinemas, restaurants, swimming pools and even ice rinks. Some shopping centres have got theme parks inside. You can spend a whole day there and never go outside.

But not everyone is happy. Some people say these giant centres are bad for small, local shops like the baker's or the newsagent's. What do you think — is bigger always better?

✍️ Writing

A short note. Напиши записку другу (5–6 предложений): где ты сейчас, что делаешь и что покупаешь. Используй Present continuous.

Проверь: am/is/are + -ing, правильное написание -ing, фразы для покупок.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Black Friday: good deal or big problem?

Once a year, in November, there is a special shopping day called Black Friday. On this day, shops reduce their prices, and many things are on sale.

On Black Friday, millions of people go shopping or buy things online. They look for cheap TVs, clothes and phones. Some shops are so busy that people wait outside all night to be first!

But some people don't like Black Friday. They say we buy too many things we don't need, and it is bad for the environment. 'I never shop on Black Friday,' says one teenager. 'The best deal is to buy nothing!'
