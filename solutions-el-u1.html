🎧 Аудирование

A family photo

Послушай диалог, потом ответь на вопросы.

Tom: Hi Anna! Is this a photo of your family?
Anna: Yes, it is. This is my mum, Elena, and my dad, Victor.
Tom: And who's that? Your sister?
Anna: No, that's my aunt, Maria. She's my mum's sister.
Tom: Have you got any brothers or sisters?
Anna: Yes, I've got one brother. His name's Pavel. He's twelve.
Tom: What does your dad do?
Anna: He works in a hospital. He's a doctor.
Tom: Cool! And your mum?
Anna: She's a teacher. She teaches maths at my school.
Tom: You've got a nice family, Anna!

🎧 Аудирование 2

My grandfather

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Jack: Who's this in the photo, Ben?
Ben: That's my grandfather, Robert. He's seventy.
Mia: Does he live with you?
Ben: No, he doesn't. He lives in the countryside with my grandmother.
Mia: What does he do?
Ben: He's retired now, but he was a farmer. He's got a lot of animals.
Jack: Do you visit them often?
Ben: Yes, we do. We go there every summer. I love it!

🔤 Word Skills (единственное и множественное число)

Неправильные формы множественного числа: child→children, man→men, woman→women, person→people.

📕 Чтение

My brother and me

My name's Kate and I've got one brother, Sam. He's fifteen and I'm thirteen. We live with our parents in a small town.

Sam and I are very different. He gets up early and does his homework every evening, but I get up late and I never tidy my room! Sam plays football at the weekend, and I dance and play the guitar. We often argue about the TV, but he's still my best friend.

📕 Чтение 2 (A profile)

All about my family

Hi! I'm Marco and I live in Italy. There are five people in my family: my parents, my two sisters and me. My mum's name is Sofia and she works in a shop.

My dad, Luca, is a chef and he cooks in a restaurant. My sisters, Bianca and Chiara, go to a different school. We haven't got any pets, but my grandparents have got a big dog. At the weekend we visit them and I play with the dog!

🎯 Exam Skills Trainer

Reading: A big family

The Garcia family is very big. Mr and Mrs Garcia have got six children and fourteen grandchildren! Every Sunday, the whole family meets at their grandparents' house for lunch.

The children play in the garden and the adults cook and talk. Grandma Garcia makes a big cake for everyone. 'I love Sundays,' says Grandpa Garcia. 'The house is noisy, but a big family is a happy family!'
