🎧 Аудирование

The job interview

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Mr Blake: Come in, Sofia. Please sit down. So, have you ever worked in a café before?
Sofia: Yes, I have. I worked in a small café last summer.
Mr Blake: Great. What did you do there?
Sofia: I served customers and helped in the kitchen. I've just finished a barista course, too.
Mr Blake: Excellent. Have you got any other experience?
Sofia: I've also worked as a sales assistant, so I'm good with people.
Mr Blake: Why do you want this job?
Sofia: It's my dream to become a chef one day, and this is a great first step.
Mr Blake: I like your ambition. Can you start next Monday?
Sofia: Yes, definitely! Thank you so much.
Mr Blake: Welcome to the team, Sofia. We'll send you the details tonight.
Sofia: That's wonderful. I won't let you down!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

Careers advice at school

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Mr Reed: OK, you two. Have you thought about jobs for the future?
Ella: A bit. I've always loved animals, so maybe I'll be a vet.
Mr Reed: Great choice, Ella. Have you ever done any work experience?
Ella: Not yet, but I've helped at an animal shelter.
Mr Reed: Perfect — put that on your CV. What about you, Sam?
Sam: I'm not sure. I've never really had a dream job.
Mr Reed: That's fine. What are you good at?
Sam: I'm good at fixing things. I've already repaired three bikes this month!
Mr Reed: Then maybe engineering or being a mechanic. Let's find you some work experience.
Sam: That sounds cool, actually. Thanks, Mr Reed.

📕 Чтение

From dishwasher to chef

Marco always dreamed of cooking. When he was sixteen, he got his first job washing dishes in a busy restaurant. It was hard work, but he watched the chefs carefully and learned everything he could.

After two years, the head chef gave him a chance to cook. Marco has never looked back. He has now worked in five countries and has just opened his own small restaurant. "I haven't finished learning," he says. "A good chef never stops."

📕 Чтение 2 (Jobs that didn't exist before)

Jobs that didn't exist before

The world of work is changing fast. Twenty years ago, nobody had heard of a "social media manager" or an "app developer". Today, thousands of people do these jobs every day.

Experts say that many of today's students will do jobs that don't exist yet. So what should you do? They advise learning skills that computers can't copy easily: working in a team, solving problems and being creative.

The message is simple. Don't just choose one job for life. Stay curious, keep learning, and be ready to change. Your dream job might not have a name — yet!

✍️ Writing

A job application. Напиши короткое письмо-заявку (7–8 предложений): на какую работу подаёшь, какой у тебя опыт (Present Perfect: I've worked…, I've done…), твои сильные стороны и почему хочешь эту работу.

Проверь: Present Perfect для опыта, Past Simple с датами, apply for, good at + -ing.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: The changing world of work

A hundred years ago, most people did one job for their whole life. A person often worked in the same factory or on the same farm from the age of fifteen until they were old. Today, the world of work is very different.

Experts say that a young person starting work now may change jobs many times, and might do several jobs that do not even exist yet. Because of this, schools are trying to teach skills that will always be useful: how to solve problems, how to work in a team, and how to keep learning after school ends.

This can sound frightening, but it is also exciting. It means you are not stuck with one choice forever. If your first job is not right for you, there will be many other chances. The most important thing, experts say, is to stay curious and never stop learning.

Multiple choice — выбери правильный вариант
