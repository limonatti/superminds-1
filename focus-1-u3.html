🎧 Аудирование

A new job

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Zoe: Hi Leo! You look busy. What are you doing?
Leo: I'm writing emails to new clients. I've got a new job!
Zoe: Congratulations! What do you do now?
Leo: I work as a designer for a small company. I usually work from home.
Zoe: Nice. Do you like your boss?
Leo: Yes, she's great. Right now she's helping me with a difficult project.
Zoe: Is it a full-time job?
Leo: Part-time, actually — three days a week. It's perfect for me.

📕 Чтение

Unusual jobs

Most people work in offices, shops or schools, but some jobs are very unusual. A professional sleeper tests beds in hotels. A food taster tries new products and says if they are good.

These workers usually don't wear a uniform and often work part-time. They say the best thing about their job is that every day is different.

💬 How to… говорить о работе

What do you do? — I work as a… — кем работаешь
I work for… / I work in… — компания / сфера
It's a full-time / part-time job.
