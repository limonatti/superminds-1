🎧 Аудирование

Filling in a form

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Ben: Hi, I'm Ben, the new club secretary. I need to fill in this form — what's your surname?
Olga: It's Petrova. P-E-T-R-O-V-A.
Ben: Thanks. And your first name?
Olga: Olga.
Ben: How old are you, Olga?
Olga: I'm fifteen. My date of birth is the third of May.
Ben: Where are you from?
Olga: I'm from Russia, but I live here now, on Green Street.
Ben: Have you got a phone number for the club?
Olga: Yes. It's oh-seven-double-five, three-two-one.
Ben: Perfect. And why do you want to join the drama club?
Olga: I love acting, and I want to make new friends.
Ben: Great answer! That's everything. Nice to meet you, Olga!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

First day: meeting new people

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Olga: Hi everyone! I'm Olga. I'm new here.
Tom: Welcome, Olga! I'm Tom. This is my friend Ravi.
Ravi: Hi Olga, nice to meet you. Where are you from?
Olga: I'm from Russia. And you, Ravi?
Ravi: I'm from India, but I've lived here for two years.
Mia: Hi! I'm Mia, Tom's sister. Have you got any brothers or sisters?
Olga: Yes, I've got a twin brother. He goes to a different school.
Mia: Cool! We usually have lunch together at one o'clock. Come with us!
Olga: Thanks, I'd love to. See you at one!

📕 Чтение

My best friend

This is my best friend, Marek. He's sixteen and he's from Poland. Marek is tall and good-looking, with short, dark, wavy hair.

He's got one brother and a twin sister. Every day he goes to school by bike and after school he usually plays basketball. He's really friendly and I'm lucky to have him as a friend.

📕 Чтение 2 (A message from a new penfriend)

A message from a new penfriend

Hi! My name's Lucas and I'm from Brazil. I'm fourteen years old and I live in a big city with my mum, my dad and my little sister.

I'm quite tall and I've got short, curly, dark hair. In my free time I play football and I love photography. My favourite subject at school is English — that's why I'm writing to you!

Please write back and tell me about yourself. What do you look like? What do you do in your free time? I can't wait to hear from you!

✍️ Writing

Personal profile. Напиши короткий профиль о себе (5–6 предложений): имя, возраст, откуда ты, внешность (I've got…), семья и что ты обычно делаешь после школы (Present Simple).

Проверь себя: есть ли -s у he/she, правильные краткие формы (I'm, she's got), и заглавные буквы у имён и национальностей.

О себе. Заполни анкету о себе (имя, фамилия, возраст, дата рождения, национальность, внешность) и напиши 5 предложений о своём обычном дне.
