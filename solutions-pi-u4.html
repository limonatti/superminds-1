🎧 Аудирование

Planning a beach clean-up

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Ruby: Dan, our club is going to clean the beach on Saturday. Are you coming?
Dan: Sure! What time are we meeting?
Ruby: We're meeting at the car park at nine. Bring gloves and bags.
Dan: Good idea. Do you think it will rain?
Ruby: The app says it won't. It's going to be sunny all morning.
Dan: Perfect. What are we going to do with all the rubbish?
Ruby: We're going to recycle the plastic and the cans.
Dan: Nice. If we collect a lot, will the newspaper write about us?
Ruby: Maybe! If they do, more people will help next time.
Dan: That's the plan. Small actions make a big difference.
Ruby: Exactly. Oh, and I'll bring water for everyone.
Dan: Great. I'll tell the others tonight. See you Saturday!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

Radio: our changing weather

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Host: Welcome back. Today Dr Green is answering your questions about climate change. Hello!
Dr Green: Hello, happy to help.
Host: Doctor, is our weather really changing?
Dr Green: Yes. We're seeing more floods, droughts and hurricanes than fifty years ago.
Host: We have a caller. Alex, you're on the air.
Alex (caller): Hi! What can one teenager do? It feels too big for me.
Dr Green: Good question, Alex. If you use less plastic and save energy at home, it really helps. And you can tell your friends.
Host: So small actions matter?
Dr Green: Absolutely. If millions of people change a little, we will protect the planet together.
Alex (caller): That makes me feel better. Thanks, Dr Green!
Dr Green: You're welcome, Alex. Keep going!

📕 Чтение

Our green school

At our school, we care about the planet. Last year we started a "green team", and now we recycle all our paper and plastic. We also turn off the lights when we leave a room.

This term we are going to plant a small garden behind the science block. If it grows well, we will grow our own vegetables for the canteen. Our teacher says that if every school does something small, together we will make a big difference.

📕 Чтение 2 (Plastic in the ocean)

Plastic in the ocean

Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic end up in the ocean. Bottles, bags and packaging float for years and break into tiny pieces. Fish and birds eat them, and this is very dangerous for wildlife.

The good news is that people are acting. Some countries have banned plastic bags, and volunteers clean beaches every weekend. Scientists are also inventing new materials that disappear safely.

What can you do? If you carry a reusable bottle and bag, you will use much less plastic. It sounds small, but if everyone does it, the ocean will be cleaner for the next generation.

✍️ Writing

A plan to help the planet. Напиши 7–8 предложений: какую эко-проблему ты видишь, что вы going to сделать в классе/дома, и 2 условия (If we…, we'll…). Используй will для прогноза и be going to для плана.

Проверь: после if — present simple; be going to для планов; связки because/so.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: Power from nature

For most of history, people have burned coal, oil and gas to make energy. These fuels are cheap and powerful, but they also cause pollution and make climate change worse. Today many countries are turning to a cleaner idea: renewable energy.

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that never run out, such as the sun, the wind and water. Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity, while huge wind turbines use moving air. In some sunny or windy places, this clean energy is now cheaper than energy from oil or gas.

Renewable energy is not perfect. The sun does not always shine and the wind does not always blow, so scientists are working on better batteries to store the power. But most experts agree that if we want a healthy planet, we must use much more energy from nature and much less from fossil fuels.
