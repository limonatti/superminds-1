🎧 Аудирование

Finding the way

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Tourist: Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
Local: Go straight on, then take the second street on the left.
Tourist: OK. Is it far?
Local: No, it's about five minutes. The station is opposite the museum.
Tourist: Great. And is there a bank near here?
Local: Yes, there's one next to the post office, between the library and the cinema.
Tourist: Thank you very much!
Local: You're welcome. Have a nice day!

🎧 Аудирование 2

Town or country?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Jake: I love living in the city. It's more exciting than the countryside.
Mia: Really? I think the countryside is better. It's quieter and cleaner.
Jake: But the city has the best shops, cinemas and restaurants.
Mia: That's true, but the city is more crowded and more expensive.
Jake: Maybe. The countryside is more beautiful, I agree.
Mia: And it's the most relaxing place to live. I feel happier there.

📕 Чтение

Unusual cities

Some cities in the world are very unusual. Venice, in Italy, has no roads for cars. Instead, people travel by boat along canals. It is one of the most romantic cities in the world, but also one of the most crowded with tourists.

Another unusual place is a city built completely underground in Australia, called Coober Pedy. It is so hot outside that people live, work and even go to church below the ground, where it is cooler.

Perhaps the most surprising is a 'floating' village on a lake in Cambodia. The houses, shops and schools all float on the water, and everyone travels by boat. These cities show that people can live in amazing and different ways.

📕 Чтение 2

The best city in the world?

My name's Leo and I live in a small town, but last summer I visited a big capital city for the first time. It was the most exciting week of my life!

The city was much bigger and busier than my town. The buildings were taller, the streets were more crowded, and the shops were more expensive. There was a fantastic museum — the biggest I have ever seen — and the food was more delicious than at home.

But after a week, I was happy to go home. My town is smaller and quieter, but it's friendlier, and for me that's the most important thing.

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

An article. Напиши статью о своём городе (5–6 предложений): что там есть, сравни его с другим местом (bigger, quieter…), назови самое лучшее место (the best…) и посоветуй, что посмотреть.

Проверь: comparatives (than), superlatives (the most / the -est), предлоги места.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

The world's happiest cities

Every year, researchers try to find the world's happiest cities. But what makes a city a good place to live? It is not always the biggest or the richest city.

The happiest cities usually have clean air, safe streets and plenty of green parks. Public transport is cheap and easy to use, so people don't need cars. There are also good schools and hospitals, and people feel close to their neighbours.

Interestingly, the most expensive cities are not always the happiest. A smaller city with friendly people and beautiful nature can be a much better place to live than a huge, crowded capital. Happiness, it seems, is about more than money.
