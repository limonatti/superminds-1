🎧 Аудирование

The mix-up

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Mia: You'll never guess what happened. I sent a message about my boss… to my boss.
Dan: Oh no. What did it say?
Mia: That his meetings were so long that I wanted to sleep. I meant to send it to Anna!
Dan: What did he do?
Mia: He called me to his office. I was sure I'd lose my job.
Dan: And?
Mia: He laughed! He said that everyone thought his meetings were too long, and he promised to make them shorter.
Dan: So your mistake fixed the problem. Maybe I should text my boss too!

📕 Чтение

Talking without words

Experts say that most of a message is not in the words at all. Our face, our hands and the sound of our voice carry huge amounts of meaning. That is why a joke that is funny face to face can start a fight in a text message: the reader can't hear the smile behind it.

Emojis were invented partly to solve this problem — a small 🙂 can show that you're joking. But nothing fully replaces real contact. However easy it is to send a quick message, we still need to catch up in person to really feel on the same page with the people we care about.
