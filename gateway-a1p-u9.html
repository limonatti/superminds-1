🎧 Аудирование

At the safari park

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Ben: Look at that animal, Zoe! It's running so fast!
Zoe: That's a cheetah. It's the fastest animal in the world!
Ben: Is it faster than a horse?
Zoe: Much faster! A cheetah is faster than almost every animal.
Ben: And look at the elephant over there. It's enormous.
Zoe: Yes, it's the biggest animal in the park. But the whale in the ocean is even bigger.
Ben: Which animal is more dangerous, the lion or the rhino?
Zoe: Good question! I think the rhino is more dangerous when it's angry.
Ben: This safari park is amazing. It's better than the zoo!

🎧 Аудирование 2

Have you ever…?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Lily: We're planning a trip. Have you ever been to a rainforest, Sam?
Sam: No, I haven't. But I've been to the mountains many times.
Lily: Have you ever seen a wild animal?
Sam: Yes, I have! I've seen a bear in Canada. It was scary!
Lily: Wow! I've never seen a bear. But I've swum in the ocean with dolphins.
Sam: That's amazing! Have you ever climbed a mountain?
Lily: No, I've never climbed one. Maybe on this trip!
Sam: Great idea. Let's find the highest mountain and climb it together!

🔤 Word Skills (работа со словом)

Сравнение: -er/-est (big→bigger→biggest), more/most для длинных слов.

📕 Чтение

The amazing Arctic tern

Many birds fly south in winter, but one small bird makes the longest journey of any animal on Earth: the Arctic tern.

Every year, this little bird flies from the Arctic in the north to the Antarctic in the south — and then back again! That is about 70,000 kilometres. In its life, an Arctic tern flies the same distance as three trips to the Moon.

How does such a small bird do it? Its wings are perfect for long flights, and it follows the sun to find the best weather and the most food. The Arctic tern shows us that the smallest animals can do the most incredible things.

📕 Чтение 2

A river of plastic

Our oceans are in danger. Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic go into the sea, and it hurts the animals that live there.

Fish, whales and birds sometimes eat small pieces of plastic because they think it is food. This makes them ill. Scientists have found plastic in the stomachs of animals in the deepest parts of the ocean.

But there is good news. Around the world, people are cleaning beaches and rivers, and clever inventors have built machines to take plastic out of the water. We can all help too: use less plastic, recycle more, and never leave rubbish on the beach. Together, we can protect the most beautiful places on our planet.

✅ True or False (по текстам юнита)

💬 How to… сравнивать и говорить об опыте

… is bigger/faster than … — больше/быстрее, чем…
… is the biggest/best … — самый большой/лучший…
Have you ever…? — ты когда-нибудь…?
I've never… / I've been to… — я никогда не… / я был в…

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

The lungs of our planet

The Amazon rainforest is one of the most important places on Earth. People sometimes call it 'the lungs of the planet'.

The Amazon is enormous — it is bigger than most countries. It is home to more animals and plants than anywhere else in the world. Scientists have found thousands of species there, and there are surely many more that we haven't discovered yet.

But the rainforest is in danger, because people cut down the trees. This is bad for the animals and bad for all of us, because the trees clean our air. The good news is that more and more people now understand the problem, and they are working hard to protect this amazing green world.
