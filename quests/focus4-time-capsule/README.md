# The Time Capsule

Focus 4 **Second Edition**, **Unit 1.2 Grammar: Present and past habits**, Student's Book p. 6, B2. It follows page 6 and is available from the top of Unit 1. It does not replace the textbook exercises or claim to cover the whole unit.

Three stations, six new challenges, nineteen checked responses. The tasks distinguish past states, repeated actions and single events; use will/would for characteristic actions; practise continuous forms with always; and end with an archive interview and a 45–70-word personal or fictional note. The open tasks use criteria and teacher feedback, without automatic grading. Typing is required only for personal writing; the interview also supports oral practice.

The original recording is Pearson SB track **1.06**, from this same subsection, with real human voices, replay and download controls. Its transcript is from Teacher's Book printed p. 197 / PDF p. 219 and is included in the web version and printable booklet. No video availability is claimed here; the separate video audit remains queued after the quest work.

The generated four-panel illustration supplies the visual evidence for challenge 2. The archive notes explicitly establish the time frame: a still picture alone cannot prove a habit. Exact image prompt and provenance are in `assets/image-prompts.md`.
