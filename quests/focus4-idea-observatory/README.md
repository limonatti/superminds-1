# The Idea Observatory

Focus 4 **Second Edition**, **Unit 1.4 Reading: Hidden talents**, Student's Book pp. 8–9, B2. Page 9 contains the reading text but no separately numbered exercises in the platform. The entry therefore follows all ten exercises on page 8, which work with the complete 8–9 spread, before 1.5 on page 10. It is also visible at the top of Unit 1.

Three stations, six authored challenges, twenty-two checked responses: a new gapped exhibition guide, supported inferences, illustrated brain expressions, source collocations, pronoun references and a personal visitor conversation. The story and questions are original; no textbook exercise has been duplicated or replaced. The optional reference panel shows the learner's own saved sentence choices, including incomplete gaps; it never substitutes an answer key. Sentence cards remain in the page flow so they cannot cover the mobile exercise.

The generated four-panel illustration is evidence for the expressions in Challenge 3. Its exact prompt is in `assets/image-prompts.md`. Original human recording **SB 1.15** accompanies the brain expressions from p. 8, exercise 8; the course phrase list and meanings are available with the recording and in the booklet. No new scientific claims are taught by this quest.
