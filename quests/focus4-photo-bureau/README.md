# The Photo Bureau

Focus 4 **Second Edition**, **Unit 1.6 Speaking: Describing a photo**, Student's Book p. 11, B2. The entry follows page 11 and appears at the top of Unit 1.

Three stations, six original challenges and twenty checked responses. Learners match tentative feeling captions to visible details, separate observation from speculation, choose forms for three time frames and identify an opinion with a reason. Two open speaking tasks then develop a photo report and a personal learning opinion. Both offer oral practice or written notes and explicit criteria; neither receives a fake automatic language grade.

The four generated photographic scenes are teaching illustrations, not documentary photographs. They supply evidence for Challenges 1, 2 and 5. Their exact prompt is in `assets/image-prompts.md`. Original human audio **SB 1.19** comes from this same subsection; the transcript is in Teacher's Book printed p. 198 / PDF p. 220. Controls, repeat, download and transcript are available.
