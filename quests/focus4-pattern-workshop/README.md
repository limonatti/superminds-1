# The Pattern Workshop

Focus 4 **Second Edition**, **Unit 1.5 Grammar: Verb patterns**, Student's Book p. 10, B2. The entry follows all page-10 exercises and is also visible at the top of Unit 1.

Three stations, six original challenges and twenty-eight checked responses cover four verb-pattern groups, object constructions, changes in meaning after remember/forget/stop/try, and complete versus ongoing actions after perception verbs. Reusable pattern cards, contextual gap cards, picture captions and meaning choices precede an oral or written learning-goal conversation. The open task stores notes and criteria without automatically grading the language.

The four generated scenes supply the evidence for captions in Challenge 3. Exact image prompt: `assets/image-prompts.md`. Original human audio **SB 1.17**, Part 2 of the subsection's podcast, supports Challenge 4. Its completed script comes from SB p. 10, exercise 5, and is available in the player and PDF. Questions test the speaker's grammar and intended meaning.
