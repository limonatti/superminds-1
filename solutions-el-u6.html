🎧 Аудирование

A trip to the safari park

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Ben: How was your trip to the safari park yesterday?
Zoe: It was amazing! We saw so many animals.
Ben: Really? What did you see?
Zoe: We watched two lions, some elephants and a giraffe. The lions were huge!
Ben: Wow. Were you scared?
Zoe: A little. A monkey jumped onto our car! But it was funny, not scary.
Ben: Could you take photos?
Zoe: Yes, I took hundreds! The elephants walked right next to us.
Ben: That sounds fantastic. I want to go too!

🎧 Аудирование 2

A wild experience

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Grandpa: When I was young, I lived near a forest.
Nina: Really? Were there any wild animals?
Grandpa: Yes! One morning, a family of deer walked through our garden.
Nina: That's amazing! Weren't you afraid?
Grandpa: No. They were beautiful and calm. I could watch them for hours.
Nina: Did you have a camera?
Grandpa: No, cameras were expensive then. But I still remember it clearly.

📕 Чтение

Stranger than fiction

Some true animal stories sound impossible. In 2005, a group of hippos in Africa saved a baby antelope from a crocodile and pushed it safely to the river bank.

In another story, a dolphin helped some lost swimmers. It swam around them in circles for a long time, keeping sharks away, until a boat arrived and rescued them.

Perhaps the strangest story is about a cat that walked 300 kilometres back to its old home after its family moved to a new city. Nobody knows how the cat found the way. These stories show that animals can surprise us in amazing ways.

📕 Чтение 2

My favourite animal

Last summer, I visited my uncle, who works at a wildlife park. It was the best week of my holidays. My favourite animal was a young elephant called Nala.

Every morning, I helped to clean her area and give her food. She was very friendly and she loved fruit. Once, she took an apple straight from my hand with her trunk! I wasn't scared at all.

In the afternoons, we watched the other animals: there were lions, giraffes and monkeys. I learned a lot about how to protect wild animals. I want to work with animals when I'm older.

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

A postcard. Напиши открытку другу о поездке в парк дикой природы (5–6 предложений) в прошедшем времени: где ты был, каких животных видел, что они делали и как ты себя чувствовал.

Проверь: Past simple (-ed, was/were, could), связки (and, but, then), начало и конец открытки.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Bringing animals back

A hundred years ago, some wild animals almost disappeared from Europe. People hunted them, and their forests were cut down. But today, scientists are helping some of these animals to return.

Wolves, for example, are coming back to countries where they were gone for many years. Beavers, which build dams in rivers, were also brought back, and now they help to create new homes for fish and birds.

This work is not always easy. Some farmers worry about their animals, and it costs a lot of money. But many people believe that a world with more wild animals is a healthier and more beautiful world for everyone.
