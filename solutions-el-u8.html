🎧 Аудирование

Weekend plans

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Max: What are you going to do this weekend?
Lucy: I'm going to play tennis on Saturday morning. What about you?
Max: I'm not sure. Maybe I'll go swimming.
Lucy: Why don't you come with me? We're going to have a match at the club.
Max: OK, good idea! Are you going to win?
Lucy: I hope so! I practise every day, so I think I'll play well.
Max: Nice. I'll bring my racket and watch, then.
Lucy: Perfect. See you on Saturday at ten!

🎧 Аудирование 2

Making an arrangement

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Coach: There's a big race in town next month. Are you going to enter?
Sam: I'd like to, but I'm not sure I'm fast enough.
Coach: You are! If you train hard, you'll do really well.
Sam: OK. I'll start running every morning before school.
Coach: Good. And I think you'll need new running shoes.
Sam: You're right. I'm going to buy some this weekend.
Coach: Great. I'm sure you'll be ready.

📕 Чтение

Against the odds

Some athletes become champions even when everything is against them. Bethany Hamilton was a young surfer who lost her arm in a shark attack when she was only thirteen.

Many people thought she would never surf again. But Bethany didn't give up. Just one month after the attack, she was back in the water, learning to surf with one arm. A few years later, she was winning competitions again.

Her story shows that with courage and hard work, people can do amazing things. 'I don't need easy,' she once said. 'I just need possible.'

📕 Чтение 2

My sporty family

Everyone in my family loves sport. My dad is a marathon runner, and every Sunday he goes running for two hours. My mum teaches yoga at the local gym.

Next summer, we're going to try something new together: we're going to learn to surf on holiday. I'm a bit nervous because I'm not a strong swimmer, but my sister says I'll be fine. She thinks I'll love it.

I'm going to practise swimming before we go. I hope I'll be ready. Who knows — maybe one day I'll be a champion surfer!

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

An informal letter. Напиши другу неформальное письмо (5–6 предложений): расскажи о своих планах на каникулы (I'm going to…), сделай пару предсказаний (I think I'll…) и пригласи друга присоединиться.

Проверь: be going to (планы), will (предсказания), начало и конец письма.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Sport for everyone

Sport is not only for professional athletes. Doctors say that everyone should be active, because sport keeps our bodies and minds healthy.

You don't need to be a champion or have expensive equipment. Walking, cycling to school or dancing in your room all count as exercise. Team sports like football or basketball are also a great way to make friends and learn to work together.

In the future, schools are going to give students more time for sport, and cities are going to build more parks and cycle lanes. Experts believe that if we stay active, we will live longer, happier lives.
