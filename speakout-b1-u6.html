🎧 Аудирование

At the gallery

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Ella: What do you think of this painting, Nick?
Nick: Honestly? I think it's the strangest thing I've ever seen.
Ella: Really? I love it. It's more creative than the others.
Nick: Hmm. I used to hate modern art, but this one is growing on me.
Ella: Have you seen the sculpture upstairs yet?
Nick: Not yet. Is it good?
Ella: It's amazing — the best piece in the whole exhibition, in my opinion.
Nick: OK, I've just decided: let's go and see it now.

📕 Чтение

The child who drew on walls

When she was small, Mia drew on everything — walls, tables, even her shoes. Her parents were not happy, but her grandmother said, 'Let her create.' Mia used to spend hours imagining new worlds with a simple pencil.

Years later, Mia became a designer. She says the most important thing is not talent but curiosity. 'A work of art starts with a question: what if?' Modern art is not always easy to understand, and it may not be everyone's cup of tea. But, she smiles, sometimes a strange painting slowly grows on you — just like her drawings once grew on her family.
