🎧 Аудирование

A typical day

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Zoe: Tell me about a typical school day, Ben.
Ben: Well, I get up at seven and I have a shower. Then I have breakfast.
Zoe: What time do you go to school?
Ben: I go to school at eight. School starts at half past eight.
Zoe: What do you do after school?
Ben: I always do my homework first. Then I play computer games or chat online.
Zoe: Do you do any sport?
Ben: Yes, I go to the sports centre twice a week. I love basketball.
Zoe: And what time do you go to bed?
Ben: At ten o'clock. I never go to bed late on school days.

🎧 Аудирование 2

What do you do at the weekend?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Sam: What do you usually do at the weekend, Lily?
Lily: On Saturday I often go out with my friends. We go to the shopping centre or the cinema.
Sam: Do you do your homework at the weekend?
Lily: Yes, I always do it on Sunday morning. Then I'm free!
Sam: Do you do any sport?
Lily: I sometimes go to the swimming pool, but I don't like team sports.
Sam: What do you do in the evening?
Lily: I usually read or listen to music. I never play computer games.

🔤 Word Skills (работа со словом)

Present simple: he/she/it + -s (goes, watches, studies).

📕 Чтение

A day in a very busy life

Marta is thirteen, but she has got an amazing life. She is a young gymnast and she trains every day.

On a normal day, Marta gets up at half past five. She goes to the sports centre before school and trains for two hours. Then she goes to school like other students. After school, she does her homework quickly and trains again in the evening.

'I don't have much free time,' says Marta, 'but I love it. At the weekend I sometimes go out with my friends, but I never miss my training. One day I want to go to the Olympics!'

📕 Чтение 2

The town that never sleeps

Some towns are quiet in the evening, but not our town! In the centre there is a big shopping centre, two cinemas, a swimming pool and lots of restaurants.

In the day, people go to the library or the museum. In the evening, families go out to eat, and teenagers meet their friends at the sports centre or the park. On Saturdays, the shopping centre is open until eleven o'clock at night.

'I love living here,' says Omar. 'There is always something to do. I never get bored!' But some older people say the town is too busy and noisy.

💬 How to… говорить о привычках и частоте

What time do you…? — во сколько ты…?
always / usually / often / sometimes / never + глагол
How often do you…? — как часто…? — twice a week, every day
What do you do at the weekend? — что ты делаешь на выходных?

✍️ Writing

A short note. Напиши короткую записку другу (5–6 предложений) о своём обычном дне: во сколько встаёшь, что делаешь после школы и что делаешь в свободное время.

Проверь: Present simple (окончание -s), наречия частоты (always/never), предлоги времени (at, in).

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

How teenagers spend their free time

What do teenagers around the world do in their free time? A big survey gives us some interesting answers.

Most teenagers spend a lot of time online. They chat with friends, watch videos and play games. But they also do other things: about half of them do sport every week, and many young people read books or draw in their free time.

Experts say free time is important. It helps young people relax and make friends. But they also say teenagers shouldn't spend all day in front of a screen — it is good to go out, do sport and meet friends in the real world too.
