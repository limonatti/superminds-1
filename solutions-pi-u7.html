🎧 Аудирование

Saving up for a bike

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Kate: Nick, you look happy. What's up?
Nick: I've almost saved enough for a new bike! Just twenty pounds to go.
Kate: Nice! How long have you been saving?
Nick: Three months. I do a paper round and I don't waste money on sweets any more.
Kate: Impressive. If I had your willpower, I'd save more too.
Nick: Ha! It's not easy. Last week I nearly spent it all on a game.
Kate: So what stopped you?
Nick: I thought, 'If I buy this, I won't get the bike.' So I put the money back.
Kate: Smart. Could you lend me a fiver until Friday? I'm a bit short.
Nick: Sure, but pay me back — I need every penny!
Kate: Of course. I wish I were as good with money as you.
Nick: You will be. Just save a little every week — it adds up.

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

Radio: smart money tips for teens

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Host: Welcome back! Today money expert Ms Fox shares tips for teenagers. Hello!
Ms Fox: Hi! Great to be here.
Host: So, what's your number one tip?
Ms Fox: Simple: save a little every time you get money. Even ten percent adds up fast.
Host: We've got a caller. Jake, go ahead.
Jake (caller): Hi! I always spend my pocket money in one day. What can I do?
Ms Fox: Good question, Jake. Try this: keep your savings in a different place. If it's harder to reach, you'll spend less.
Host: And what about borrowing from friends?
Ms Fox: Be careful. Only borrow what you can pay back. If you don't, you might lose the friend too!
Jake (caller): That makes sense. Thanks, Ms Fox!
Ms Fox: Any time, Jake. Small habits make a big difference.

📕 Чтение

The best bargain ever

My grandma is the queen of bargains. She never pays full price for anything. Last month she wanted a warm winter coat, but the shops were too expensive for her budget.

So she waited. She checked the sales every week, and finally she found the perfect coat with a 70% discount. "If you're patient," she always says, "you can afford almost anything." She kept the receipt, of course — just in case. Now she looks great and still has money left for a coffee!

📕 Чтение 2 (A world without cash?)

A world without cash?

Not long ago, everyone paid with coins and notes. Today, many people pay with a card or a phone, and some countries are almost cashless. Is this a good thing?

There are clear advantages. Digital money is fast, and you don't lose it as easily as cash. Shops like it too, because counting money takes time. But there are problems. Some older people find apps difficult, and it's easier to overspend when you don't see real money leaving your hand.

Experts say the best idea is balance: enjoy the speed of digital payments, but always know your budget. If you can't see your money, you have to watch it even more carefully.

✍️ Writing

Advice about money. Друг тратит все карманные деньги сразу и просит совет. Напиши ответ (7–8 предложений): 3 совета (If I were you, I'd… / You should…) и что бы ты сделал(а) на его месте. Используй second conditional и I wish минимум по разу.

Проверь: If + Past Simple, … would…; borrow vs lend; I wish + Past Simple.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: Learning to be good with money

Most schools teach maths, science and languages, but few teach one very useful skill: how to manage money. Many young people leave school without ever learning how to save, budget or avoid debt.

Experts say that good money habits start early. When children get a small amount of pocket money and decide how to spend it, they learn important lessons. If they spend it all in one day, they feel the result and often plan better next time. Saving up for something special teaches patience, and it feels great to buy something with your own money.

You do not need to be rich to be good with money. The most important habits are simple: spend less than you earn, save a little every week, and think before you buy. People who learn these habits when they are young usually worry much less about money when they are older.
