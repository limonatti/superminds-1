🎧 Аудирование

The interview

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Mr Lane: So, Emma, tell me about your experience.
Emma: I've worked as a designer for six years. In my last job I led a small team of three people.
Mr Lane: Why did you leave?
Emma: The company was great, but I had to commute for two hours every day. If I work here, I'll be able to walk to the office!
Mr Lane: Do you prefer working alone or in a team?
Emma: In a team. If I worked alone all the time, I'd miss talking to people.
Mr Lane: When can you start?
Emma: I need to give my current boss two weeks' notice, so — on the first of March.

📕 Чтение

The four-day week

For a hundred years, most people have worked five days a week. But recently some companies have tried something new: a four-day week with the same pay. Employees get a longer weekend, and — surprisingly — they often get the same amount of work done.

Why does it work? With less time, people waste fewer hours in long meetings and focus on what matters. They come back rested, so a good work-life balance actually makes them more productive. Of course, it doesn't suit every job. But many workers say that if they could choose, they 'd hand in their notice before going back to five long days.
