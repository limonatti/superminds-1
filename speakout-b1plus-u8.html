🗣️ Произношение 'would have' → /wʊdəv/

В речи would have звучит как /wʊdəv/, should have — /ʃʊdəv/. Не говори 'would of' — это на письме ошибка.

🎧 Аудирование

Grandad's advice

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Dan: My grandad turned ninety yesterday. I asked him for his best life advice.
Mia: Ooh, what did he say?
Dan: When he was young, he would work seven days a week. He said he should have rested more.
Mia: Interesting. Anything else?
Dan: Yes. He said that if he hadn't gone to a dance in 1958, he would never have met my grandmother.
Mia: So his advice is… go dancing?
Dan: Exactly! Say yes to invitations. The best things happen when you least expect them.
Mia: Your grandad is a wise man. I should have asked mine the same question while I could.

📕 Чтение

The best advice I never took

Older people often say the same thing: 'If only I'd known then what I know now.' With hindsight, our mistakes look obvious, but at the time we couldn't see them. That is why advice is so hard to give — and even harder to take.

Still, some lessons are worth passing on. Say yes to opportunities. Rest more. Call the people you love. We usually learn our lesson the slow way, through our own mistakes, and that's normal. But every now and then a bad moment turns out to be a blessing in disguise — and, looking back, we feel grateful it happened at all.
