🎧 Аудирование

A witness statement

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Officer: Thanks for calling us, Lucy. Can you tell me what you saw?
Lucy: Yes. At about nine o'clock I saw a man near my neighbour's house.
Officer: What was he doing?
Lucy: He was looking through the window. When I looked again, he had disappeared.
Officer: Did you see his face?
Lucy: Not clearly. He was tall and he wore a dark hoodie.
Officer: And what happened next?
Lucy: I heard glass break, so I called you immediately.
Officer: You did the right thing. By the time we arrived, he had already run away.
Lucy: Did he steal anything?
Officer: A laptop and some cash. But thanks to you, we have good evidence.
Lucy: I hope you catch him soon.
Officer: We will. Your description is very helpful, Lucy.

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

News report: the café robbery

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Reporter: This is Tom Price with the local news. Last night, a café in the town centre was robbed. I'm here with a witness, Mrs Hall.
Mrs Hall: Yes, I was walking my dog when I saw two people run out of the café.
Reporter: What did they look like?
Mrs Hall: They were young and they had covered their faces. One was carrying a bag.
Reporter: We also have Detective Cole. Detective, what can you tell us?
Detective Cole: We believe the thieves had watched the café for several days. They knew exactly when it was empty.
Reporter: Have you made any arrests?
Detective Cole: Not yet, but we have CCTV footage and a witness. We're confident we'll catch them.
Mrs Hall: I told the police everything I remembered.
Detective Cole: And that's a great help. If anyone has information, please call us.

📕 Чтение

The detective who never gave up

Inspector Reed was famous for solving difficult cases. One winter, a valuable painting disappeared from the city museum. The thieves had planned everything carefully and had left no fingerprints.

But Reed noticed one small thing: a wet footprint near the door. He realised the thief had walked through snow before entering. After weeks of work, he found the man — a guard who had "discovered" the crime himself. The guard said he was innocent, but the evidence told a different story. Reed had solved the case again.

📕 Чтение 2 (Keeping our streets safe)

Keeping our streets safe

Most people will never be victims of a serious crime, but small steps can make a neighbourhood much safer. In many towns, people have started "neighbourhood watch" groups. Neighbours simply keep an eye on each other's homes and report anything strange.

Technology helps too. Cameras and good street lighting mean criminals are more likely to get caught. But experts say the most powerful tool is community: when people know and trust their neighbours, crime usually goes down.

So what can you do? Lock your doors, look out for others, and report problems early. If we all take small actions, our streets will be safer for everyone.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: The science of catching criminals

In the past, catching a criminal often depended on luck or on a witness who saw the crime. Today, science plays a huge part. Detectives use special tools and clever tests that did not exist a hundred years ago.

One of the most famous is the fingerprint. Every person's fingerprints are different, so a single print left at the scene can identify a thief. More recently, scientists have learned to test tiny pieces of skin or hair to find out who was there. Cameras in streets and shops also help the police to follow a suspect's movements.

However, technology is only a tool. Detectives still need to think carefully, ask the right questions and notice small details that a machine might miss. The best investigations happen when clever science and a curious human mind work together.
