🎧 Аудирование

What have you got today?

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Mia: Hi, Leo! What subjects have you got today?
Leo: I've got maths, ICT and PE. What about you?
Mia: I've got English, history and science. Have you got your calculator?
Leo: Yes, it's in my pencil case. But I haven't got my folder for history.
Mia: Don't worry, it isn't your lesson! Whose tablet is this?
Leo: It's mine. We use it in ICT.
Mia: Cool. What have we got after lunch?
Leo: We've got music. I love music!
Mia: Me too. Let's go, the bell is ringing.

🎧 Аудирование 2

Describing a friend

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Sam: Who is your new friend, Ava?
Ava: Her name's Nadia. She's in my French class.
Sam: What does she look like?
Ava: She's tall and she's got long, dark, curly hair.
Sam: Has she got brown eyes?
Ava: Yes, she has. And she's very friendly and clever.
Sam: Is that Nadia over there, with the short hair?
Ava: No, that isn't her. Nadia's hair is long!

📕 Чтение

School clubs at Green Park

At Green Park School, lessons finish at half past three, but many students stay for the after-school clubs. There is a club for everyone!

On Mondays there is a science club, where students do fun experiments. On Wednesdays there is a music club — you can play the guitar, sing or use the computers to make songs. On Fridays the art club is very popular.

'My favourite is the ICT club,' says Marco. 'We make simple games on our laptops and tablets. It's better than any lesson!' The clubs are free, and all the students say they are a great way to make new friends.

📕 Чтение 2

My best friend

My best friend is called Zoe. We are in the same class and we sit together in every lesson except PE.

Zoe is tall and she's got long, straight, fair hair and big green eyes. People say we look similar, but that isn't true — my hair is short and dark!

Zoe is very clever and hard-working. Her favourite subjects are science and maths, but she isn't good at art — like me! After school we always do our homework together and then we chat online in the evening.

💬 How to… описать человека

What does he/she look like? — как он/она выглядит?
He's/She's got + (длина)(цвет) hair. — у него/неё … волосы
He's/She's + прилагательное. (tall, friendly, clever)
Has he/she got…? — Yes, he/she has. / No, he/she hasn't.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

School uniforms around the world

In many countries, students wear a school uniform. But uniforms are very different from place to place.

In the UK, most students wear a shirt, a jumper and dark trousers or a skirt. In Japan, uniforms are famous, and some schools change them for summer and winter. In many countries in Europe, like France or Spain, students don't have to wear a uniform at all — they wear their own clothes.

Some students love their uniform because it is easy and everyone looks the same. Others think it is boring. What do you think? Is a uniform a good idea for your school?
