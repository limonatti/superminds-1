🎧 Аудирование

Planning a holiday

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Mia: I'm so excited! We're going to Italy next week.
Ben: Lucky you! How are you going to travel?
Mia: We're going to fly to Rome, and then we're going to travel by train.
Ben: What are you going to do there?
Mia: We're going to visit museums and eat lots of pizza! And we're going to the beach.
Ben: What's the weather going to be like?
Mia: It's going to be hot and sunny. So I'm going to take my sunglasses and sunscreen.
Ben: Don't forget your passport!
Mia: Ha! Of course. It's the first thing in my suitcase.

🎧 Аудирование 2

Before the trip

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Sam: Are you ready for your ski trip, Zoe?
Zoe: Almost! I'm packing my suitcase now. It's going to be very cold.
Sam: Then you should take warm clothes and gloves.
Zoe: Good idea. Do I have to take my passport?
Sam: Yes, you must. You can't travel to another country without it.
Zoe: OK. Should I take sunscreen for skiing?
Sam: Yes, you should! The sun is strong in the mountains, even when it's cold.
Zoe: I didn't know that. Thanks for the advice!
Sam: No problem. Have a great trip!

📕 Чтение

The strangest hotels in the world

When you go on holiday, you usually sleep in a normal hotel. But some hotels are very unusual!

In Sweden, there is an ice hotel. Every winter, people build it from ice and snow, and it melts in spring. You sleep in a warm sleeping bag, but the walls and even the beds are made of ice! In other places, you can sleep in a treehouse, underwater, or in an old plane.

These hotels aren't cheap, and you must book them early because they are very popular. But if you want an adventure, they are much more exciting than a normal room. Where would you like to stay?

📕 Чтение 2

How to be a good traveller

Travelling to a new country is exciting, but a good traveller always prepares well. Here is some simple advice.

Before you go, you should read a guidebook and learn a few words of the language — people love it when you try! You must take your passport, and you should check the weather so you know what clothes to pack. Don't forget things like sunscreen or an umbrella.

When you arrive, be polite and respect the local culture. You shouldn't drop rubbish or be too noisy. Try the local food, talk to people, and take lots of photos. If you follow this advice, you're going to have an amazing trip!

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Slow travel: enjoy the journey

When people go on holiday, they usually want to arrive as fast as possible. They take a plane and fly over everything below them. But now, more and more people are choosing 'slow travel'.

Slow travel means enjoying the journey, not just the destination. Instead of flying, you travel by train, boat or even bicycle. You stop in small towns, meet local people and see places that most tourists never visit.

Slow travel is also better for the planet, because trains and boats produce less pollution than planes. It takes more time, but people who try it say it is the best way to travel. 'You shouldn't rush your holiday,' says one traveller. 'The journey is part of the adventure.'
