🎧 Аудирование

The weekend match

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Mia: Ben, did you play football at the weekend?
Ben: Yes, we had a big match on Saturday.
Mia: Did you win?
Ben: We did! I scored two goals. It was amazing.
Mia: Well done! Did you go running on Sunday too?
Ben: No, I didn't. I was too tired, so I stayed home and rested.
Mia: Good idea. What about you — do you do any sport?
Ben: She goes swimming twice a week and does yoga on Fridays.

📕 Чтение

A healthy champion

Nora is sixteen and she is a swimming champion. Last year she won three medals. She trains every morning before school and does exercise in the gym twice a week.

Nora eats a healthy diet and goes to bed early. "I didn't win because I'm lucky," she says. "I won because I trained hard every day."

💬 How to… говорить о спорте

Do you do any sport? — Yes, I play/go/do…
I'm good at… / I'm not very good at…
Did you win? — Yes, we won! / No, we lost.

🏠 Домашнее задание

Мои выходные / My sporty weekend. Напиши 6–7 предложений в Past Simple: каким спортом ты занимался, куда ходил, выиграл/проиграл, как себя чувствовал. Используй минимум 3 неправильных глагола (went, did, had, won).
