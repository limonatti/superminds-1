📖 Разбор грамматики

Past simple — negative
didn't + базовый глагол (без -ed!): I didn't work.
Одинаково для всех лиц: He didn't go. They didn't like it.
Неверно: He didn't went. Верно: He didn't go.
Past simple — questions
Did + подлежащее + базовый глагол: Did you work?
wh-: What did you do? Where did she go?
Глагол в базовой форме: Did he win? (не won)
Short answers
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Did she like it? — Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
С to be: Was it hard? — Yes, it was.
Talking about jobs
What do you do? · I'd like to be a… · I'm good at…
a well-paid / badly-paid job · a full-time / part-time job
You need to be responsible / creative / hard-working.
