🎧 Аудирование

My school day

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Anna: Hi, Tim! What time do you wake up on school days?
Tim: I wake up at half past six. Then I get dressed and have breakfast.
Anna: What time do you arrive at school?
Tim: At quarter to eight. Lessons start at eight o'clock.
Anna: How many lessons do you have?
Tim: We have six lessons. My favourite subjects are biology and history.
Anna: Do you have to wear a uniform?
Tim: Yes, we do. We have to wear a white shirt and black trousers.
Anna: What time do you leave school?
Tim: At half past three. Then I do my homework and have dinner at seven.

🎧 Аудирование 2

Giving advice

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Mia: I'm always tired in the morning. What should I do?
Leo: You shouldn't go to bed late. Try to sleep more.
Mia: And I never have time for breakfast.
Leo: You should wake up ten minutes earlier. Breakfast is important.
Mia: OK. I sometimes forget my books, too.
Leo: You should check your timetable in the evening.
Mia: Thanks. That's good advice!

📕 Чтение

Dangerous journeys to school

Not all children go to school by bus or car. For some students around the world, the journey to school is long and dangerous.

In one village in China, children walk for two hours along narrow mountain paths. In parts of Colombia, some students cross a wide river on a metal cable, high above the water. In the Philippines, children sometimes use old tyres to float across a lake.

These journeys are difficult, but the students don't give up. They wake up very early, sometimes at five o'clock, and they arrive at school tired but happy. For them, education is worth the danger.

📕 Чтение 2

An unusual school

Green Valley School is not a normal school. Students don't have to wear a uniform and there are no exams. Every morning, students choose their own lessons.

On Mondays, Ben studies science and cooking. On Wednesdays, he does music and sport. He never studies maths on Fridays because he prefers art. The teachers help students, but the students make their own timetable.

'I love my school,' says Ben. 'I always want to come here. We learn a lot, but we also have fun.'

✍️ Writing

An announcement. Напиши короткое объявление о школьном мероприятии (5–6 предложений): что это (sports day / concert / school trip), когда и во сколько (on… at…), где и что нужно принести.

Проверь: предлоги времени (on Monday, at 3 o'clock), заглавные буквы, should / have to.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

The school timetable of the future

Schools are changing. In many countries, experts think the school day of the future will be very different from today.

Some schools already start later in the morning. Scientists say that teenagers need more sleep, so a later start — for example at ten o'clock — can help students learn better. Other schools have shorter lessons but more breaks, because students often can't concentrate for a long time.

Technology is important, too. In the future, students may not have to carry heavy books. They will use tablets and study some lessons at home. But most teachers agree on one thing: school is not only about subjects. It is also a place to meet friends and learn how to work together.
