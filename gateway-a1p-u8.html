🎧 Аудирование

A job interview

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Manager: Good morning. Please sit down. So, you want a summer job here?
Anna: Yes, I do. I'd like to work in your café.
Manager: Great. Did you work last summer?
Anna: Yes, I did. I worked in a shop for two months.
Manager: Did you like it?
Anna: I liked meeting people, but I didn't like the early mornings!
Manager: Ha! Why do you think you're good for this job?
Anna: I'm friendly and hard-working, and I'm good at maths, so I can use the till.
Manager: Excellent. Can you start next Monday?
Anna: Yes, of course. Thank you!

🎧 Аудирование 2

My work experience

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Mia: How was your work experience, Leo?
Leo: It was great! I worked with a mechanic in a garage.
Mia: Did you fix any cars?
Leo: Well, I didn't fix them alone, but I helped the mechanic. I learned a lot.
Mia: Was it hard work?
Leo: Yes, it was. It's a skilled job and it's quite dirty! But I enjoyed it.
Mia: Did you decide what you want to do in the future?
Leo: Not really. I liked it, but I didn't love it. Maybe I want a more creative job.

📕 Чтение

The world's most unusual jobs

When you grow up, you probably won't be a professional 'ice cream taster' or a 'panda nanny' — but these are real jobs!

Some people have very unusual work. An ice cream taster tries new flavours and says which are good. A panda nanny looks after baby pandas in China — you need to be very kind and patient. There are even people who are paid to sleep, to help scientists study how we rest!

These jobs are fun, but most of them are not well-paid, and they are difficult to get. Still, they show us that the world of work is bigger and stranger than we think.

📕 Чтение 2

A teenager with a big idea

Most teenagers spend their money, but Ben Taylor started a business when he was only fifteen.

Ben loved computers and he was good at fixing them. One day, a neighbour asked him to repair her laptop. He fixed it quickly, and she paid him. Soon, more people asked for his help. Ben didn't have an office; he just worked from his bedroom after school.

Now, three years later, Ben has a small company and two employees. 'You don't need to be old to start a business,' he says. 'You just need a good idea, and you need to be hard-working and responsible. I love being my own boss!'

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Will robots take our jobs?

Robots and computers can already do many things. So people often ask: will robots take all our jobs in the future?

It is true that machines are good at some jobs. In factories, robots build cars faster than people. Computers can check numbers and answer simple questions. In the future, some jobs, like driving, may change a lot.

But experts say we shouldn't worry too much. Robots aren't good at everything. They can't be creative, kind or friendly like people. Jobs like nurse, teacher, artist or chef need human skills that machines don't have. And when old jobs disappear, new jobs always appear. The future of work will be different — but there will still be a lot for people to do.
