🎧 Аудирование

Checking in at the hostel

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Lena: Hi, I've got a booking for two nights. The name's Lena Orlova.
Receptionist: Welcome! Let me check… yes, a bed in the six-person room. Can I see your passport?
Lena: Sure, here you are. How long have you worked here?
Receptionist: For about three years. I love meeting travellers from everywhere.
Lena: Nice! Is there a good place to go sightseeing nearby?
Receptionist: Definitely. The old castle is a ten-minute walk — it's the main landmark here.
Lena: Great. Do you do guided tours?
Receptionist: We do, every morning at ten. And breakfast is included.
Lena: Perfect. Where can I leave my luggage?
Receptionist: In the locker room, just there. Here's your key. Enjoy your stay, Lena!
Lena: Thank you so much. This place used to be a school, didn't it?
Receptionist: Well spotted! Yes, it did. Now it's a home for backpackers.

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

City tour with a guide

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Guide: Good morning, everyone! Welcome to the old town. Has anyone been here before?
Mia: No, it's my first time. How old is this square?
Guide: Great question, Mia. It's been the centre of the city for over 600 years.
Tom: Wow. And what's that tall building?
Guide: That's the clock tower, our most famous landmark. People used to climb it to watch for fires.
Mia: Can we go up?
Guide: Yes, later. First, follow me to the market — it's off the beaten track and full of local food.
Tom: How long have you been a guide?
Guide: For fifteen years. I've never got tired of this city. Right, let's explore!
Mia: This is going to be a great day.

📕 Чтение

A village that tourism changed

Thirty years ago, Rila was a quiet fishing village. People used to catch fish and grow vegetables, and very few visitors came. Then a travel magazine called it "the most beautiful village in the country".

Since then, everything has changed. Hotels and cafés have opened, and thousands of tourists visit every summer. Many young people now work in tourism instead of fishing. Some villagers love the new life; others miss the quiet days. "We have gained a lot," says one shop owner, "but we have also lost something."

📕 Чтение 2 (Travel that helps, not harms)

Travel that helps, not harms

More people are travelling than ever before. That is exciting, but it can also harm the places we love. Too many tourists can damage nature, fill streets with rubbish and make life hard for local people.

"Responsible tourism" is a better way to travel. It means small choices: stay in local guesthouses, buy from local shops, use less water, and respect the culture. Many travellers now choose quieter places instead of crowded resorts.

The idea is simple. If we travel with care, the beautiful places we visit will still be beautiful for the people who come after us.

✍️ Writing

A travel blog post. Напиши пост о поездке (7–8 предложений): куда ездил(а), как долго был(а) там (for/since), что раньше было иначе или что ты раньше думал(а) (used to), и что понравилось. Используй Present Perfect и Past Simple.

Проверь: used to для прошлых привычек, for/since с Present Perfect.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: The age of travel

Not long ago, travelling to another country was rare and expensive. Most people spent their holidays close to home. Today, cheap flights and the internet have made the world feel much smaller, and millions of people travel abroad every year.

This has brought many good things. Travel helps people learn about other cultures, try new food and make friends from around the world. Tourism also brings money and jobs to many towns and cities that need it.

But there is another side. In some famous places, there are now so many tourists that local people find it hard to enjoy their own city. Long queues, high prices and crowded streets are common. Because of this, some experts say we should travel more slowly, visit quieter places, and always respect the people who live there.
