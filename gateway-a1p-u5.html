🎧 Аудирование

Can you help me?

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Mia: Dad, can you help me? My laptop doesn't work.
Dad: Sure. Can you turn it on?
Mia: Yes, I can, but I can't log on. I don't remember my password!
Dad: OK, don't worry. Click on 'forgot password' and check your email.
Mia: Oh, I can see it now. Thanks! Now I want to print my homework.
Dad: Is the printer on? Click on 'print' and choose the printer.
Mia: It's printing! You're a computer expert, Dad.
Dad: Not really! But remember: always save your work and never share your password.

🎧 Аудирование 2

My favourite website

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Ben: What's your favourite website, Zoe?
Zoe: I love a website about animals. You can watch videos and read a blog.
Ben: Do you like reading blogs?
Zoe: Yes, I do! I like learning new things. What about you?
Ben: I like playing games online, but I don't like social networks very much.
Zoe: Really? Why not?
Ben: People spend too much time on them. I prefer doing sport in the real world!
Zoe: That's a good point. But be careful — some websites have viruses.

📕 Чтение

A teenage app maker

Most teenagers use apps every day, but sixteen-year-old Amina makes them. She started when she was just twelve years old.

'At first, I couldn't write any code,' says Amina. 'But I watched videos online and read a blog by a young programmer. Now I can make simple games and apps.' Her most popular app helps students organise their homework.

Amina says anyone can learn to code. 'You need a computer and lots of patience. When something doesn't work, don't give up — try again! Coding is difficult, but it's also really fun.'

📕 Чтение 2

Stay safe online

The Internet is amazing, but it is important to stay safe. Here are some simple rules.

First, use a strong password and never share it with other people — not even your best friend. Second, don't click on strange links or emails, because they can have a virus. Third, be careful on social networks: don't put your home address or phone number online.

Finally, remember that not everything on the Internet is true. If you read something surprising, check it on another website. If you follow these rules, you can enjoy the Internet safely.

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Life before the Internet

Today it is hard to imagine life without the Internet. But only thirty years ago, most people didn't have it at home.

Before the Internet, people couldn't send emails or chat online. If you wanted information, you went to the library and read books. To talk to a friend in another country, you wrote a letter or made an expensive phone call. Photos weren't digital — you took them to a shop to print.

Life was slower, but it wasn't always worse. People say they read more books and spent more time outside. Today the Internet helps us do many things quickly, but it is still good to log off sometimes and enjoy the real world.
