🎧 Аудирование

A day in the mountains

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Sara: Jack, what were you doing at six o'clock yesterday? I called you.
Jack: Sorry, I was hiking in the mountains. There was no signal.
Sara: Cool! Was it a hard walk?
Jack: Yes! While we were climbing, it suddenly started to rain.
Sara: Oh no. What did you do?
Jack: We put up our tent quickly and waited. We nearly ran out of water!
Sara: Scary. Did you make it to the top?
Jack: We did, in the end. The view was amazing — it was a real adventure.

📕 Чтение

Lost in the cave

Last year, two friends went exploring in a large cave. They were walking deep inside when their torch suddenly stopped working. It was completely dark and they couldn't find the path.

They didn't panic. While one friend was looking for the exit, the other was calling for help. After three hours, a rescue team found them. It was frightening, but they survived — and they will never go into a cave without two torches again!

💬 How to… рассказать историю

It all started when… / Suddenly…
While we were…, … — фон + событие
In the end… / Luckily…

💬 Говорим (скажи вслух — по-английски)

✍️ Writing

An adventure story. Напиши историю (7–8 предложений) о приключении: где ты был(а), что делал(а) (Past Continuous как фон), что вдруг случилось (Past Simple), и чем всё закончилось. Используй while, when, suddenly, in the end, luckily.

Проверь: was/were + -ing, правильные Past Simple, связки времени.

🏠 Домашнее задание

My adventure. Опиши реальное или выдуманное приключение в Past Simple/Continuous (6–7 предложений). Мин. 3 слова из юнита (cave, tent, explore…) и связки while/when/suddenly.
