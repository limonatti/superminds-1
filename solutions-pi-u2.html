🎧 Аудирование

A day in the mountains

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Sara: Jack, what were you doing at six o'clock yesterday? I called you three times.
Jack: Sorry, Sara! I was hiking in the mountains. There was no signal up there.
Sara: Wow. Was it a hard walk?
Jack: Really hard. While we were climbing, it suddenly started to rain.
Sara: Oh no. What did you do?
Jack: We put up our tent quickly and waited. But then we nearly ran out of water!
Sara: That sounds scary. Weren't you frightened?
Jack: A little. While I was looking for the path, my friend was calling for help.
Sara: And did someone come?
Jack: Yes! A guide found us after an hour. He gave us water and showed us the way.
Sara: Thank goodness. Did you make it to the top in the end?
Jack: We did! The view was amazing — you could see three valleys.
Sara: Incredible. I want to come next time!
Jack: Deal. But bring two torches — and a lot of water!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

Radio interview: sailing across the ocean

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Host: Welcome back! Today we're speaking to Maya, who sailed across the ocean alone. Maya, welcome!
Maya: Thank you! It's great to be here.
Host: So, what were you doing when the big storm hit?
Maya: I was sleeping. Suddenly the boat started moving like crazy and water came in.
Host: That's terrifying! What did you do?
Maya: I stayed calm. While the storm was passing, I was checking the ropes and the radio.
Host: We have a caller. Leo, you're on the air.
Leo (caller): Hi Maya! Were you ever scared of the animals in the ocean?
Maya: Good question, Leo. I saw sharks once, but they weren't interested in me. The weather was the real danger.
Host: Amazing story. Any advice for young adventurers?
Maya: Yes — take risks, but always prepare well. And never travel without a map!

📕 Чтение

Lost in the cave

Last year, two friends went exploring in a large cave. They were walking deep inside when their torch suddenly stopped working. It was completely dark and they couldn't find the path.

They didn't panic. While one friend was looking for the exit, the other was calling for help. After three hours, a rescue team found them. It was frightening, but they survived — and they will never go into a cave without two torches again!

📕 Чтение 2 (The youngest to climb the peak)

The youngest to climb the peak

Last year, a fourteen-year-old girl named Aisha became the youngest person to climb a famous mountain in her country. She trained hard for two years before the big day.

The climb was not easy. While she was going up, the weather changed and it started to snow. Aisha and her team put up their tents and waited for twelve hours. Many people would have given up, but she didn't.

When the sky was clear again, they continued to the top. "I was tired and cold," Aisha said later, "but when I saw the view, I forgot everything. It was the best moment of my life."

✍️ Writing

An adventure story. Напиши историю (7–8 предложений) о приключении: где ты был(а), что делал(а) (Past Continuous как фон), что вдруг случилось (Past Simple), и чем всё закончилось. Используй while, when, suddenly, in the end, luckily.

Проверь: was/were + -ing, правильные Past Simple, связки времени.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: Why do people love dangerous sports?

Every year, millions of people around the world take part in dangerous sports such as rock climbing, skydiving and white-water rafting. To many people this seems crazy: why risk your life for fun? But scientists have some interesting answers.

One reason is a chemical in the brain that makes us feel excited and happy when we take a risk. After a difficult climb, many people feel a powerful sense of joy and success. Another reason is confidence. People who do adventure sports say that facing their fears in the mountains helps them feel braver in normal life too.

Of course, these sports can be dangerous, and accidents do happen. However, most serious climbers and divers train for years and follow strict safety rules. For them, adventure is not about being careless — it is about being well prepared and pushing their own limits.
