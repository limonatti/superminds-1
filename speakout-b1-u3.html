🎧 Аудирование

Weekend plans

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Zoe: What are you doing this weekend, Ben?
Ben: I'm meeting some friends on Saturday. We're going to explore the old town.
Zoe: Sounds fun! Do you know where the new museum is?
Ben: I'm not sure. Could you tell me if it's near the station?
Zoe: I'll look it up. Actually, why don't you come with us?
Ben: I'd love to! What time are you meeting?
Zoe: Around eleven. I think it'll be busy, so let's turn up early.
Ben: Great. I'll find out the ticket price and text you.

📕 Чтение

The city that never sleeps

Some cities feel alive at every hour. In a big city you can find out what's happening simply by walking around: markets in the morning, offices at noon, music and lights at night. Tourists often ask the same questions — 'Could you tell me where the station is?' or 'Do you know if the museum is open?'

Locals are usually happy to help. A polite question and a smile open many doors. If you plan a trip, it's a good idea to book tickets online and check the opening times. And don't be afraid to get lost: sometimes the best places are the ones you turn up at by accident.
