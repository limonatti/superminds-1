🎧 Аудирование

What shall we watch?

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Ella: What's on TV tonight, Tom?
Tom: There's a new action film and a documentary about space.
Ella: Hmm. I think documentaries are more interesting than action films.
Tom: Really? I can't stand documentaries — they're too slow for me.
Ella: This one isn't slow. It's got amazing pictures of the planets.
Tom: OK, but an action film is much more exciting.
Ella: The action film isn't as clever as a good documentary, though.
Tom: Maybe, but it's the most exciting thing on tonight!
Ella: What about the new reality show at nine?
Tom: No way — reality shows are the worst! They're not real at all.
Ella: Ha, I agree with you there. OK, let's watch the film first.
Tom: And the comedy after it? It's funnier than anything else on.
Ella: Deal. Comedy is the best way to end the evening.
Tom: Great. I'll make the popcorn — you choose the channel!

🎧 Аудирование 2 (ещё один диалог)

Vlogger Q&A: making videos

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько нужно.

Host: Today we're chatting with Zara, one of the most popular vloggers online. Hi Zara!
Zara (vlogger): Hi! Thanks for inviting me.
Host: So, is making videos as easy as it looks?
Zara (vlogger): Not at all! A ten-minute video takes me a whole day to film and edit.
Host: Wow. We have a question from a fan. Ben, go ahead.
Ben (fan): Hi Zara! What's more important — a good camera or a good idea?
Zara (vlogger): Great question, Ben. A good idea is much more important. The best videos are funny or useful, not just pretty.
Host: Any advice for people who want to start?
Zara (vlogger): Yes — start now with your phone. Don't wait for perfect equipment. And be yourself!
Ben (fan): Thanks, Zara! You're the best.
Zara (vlogger): Aw, thank you, Ben. Good luck!

📕 Чтение

Too much screen time?

These days, many teenagers spend more time on screens than ever before. They watch series, download films and play games for hours. Some people say this is bad, but it isn't as simple as that.

Good films and documentaries can teach us a lot, and they're often more exciting than a textbook. The problem is balance: if you spend too much time watching and not enough time sleeping or exercising, it isn't healthy. The best idea is to enjoy screens, but not too much.

📕 Чтение 2 (Why do we love reality shows?)

Why do we love reality shows?

Reality shows are some of the most popular programmes on TV. Millions of people watch ordinary people cook, sing, or live together in a house. But why do we love them so much?

Experts say we enjoy them because the people seem real, like us. It's exciting to guess who will win, and easy to have an opinion. Reality shows are also cheaper to make than dramas, so TV channels love them too.

Of course, not everything is as real as it looks. Producers often plan the drama, and some scenes are filmed many times. So next time you watch, remember: it's entertainment, not real life!

✍️ Writing

A film review. Напиши отзыв о фильме или сериале (7–8 предложений): название и жанр, о чём он (It's about…), что тебе понравилось/нет, сравнение с другим (better/worse/not as good as) и рекомендация. Используй превосходную степень хотя бы раз.

Проверь: -er/more, the …-est/the most, too/enough, as…as.

🎯 Exam skills · IELTS (формат экзамена)

Reading: The story of television

Today almost every home has a screen, but television is younger than you might think. The first regular TV broadcasts began less than a hundred years ago. At first, sets were expensive and the picture was black and white, so only rich families had one.

Everything changed in the 1950s and 1960s. Television became cheaper, and millions of families bought their first set. Suddenly, people all over a country could watch the same news, the same sport and the same shows at the same time. Some experts say television brought families together in the evenings; others say it stopped people talking to each other.

Now we watch on phones and laptops as well as TVs, and we choose exactly what and when to watch. Television has changed a lot, but one thing has stayed the same: people love a good story on a screen.
