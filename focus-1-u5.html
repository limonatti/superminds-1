🎧 Аудирование

Before the exam

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Tom: Ivy, I'm so nervous. We've got the maths exam tomorrow.
Ivy: Don't worry. You should revise tonight and then get some sleep.
Tom: I know. But last year I failed the test.
Ivy: That was different — you weren't well. This year you're ready.
Tom: Thanks. What must we bring to the exam?
Ivy: A pen and a ruler. And we mustn't use our phones.
Ivy: Were you good at maths at primary school?
Tom: Actually yes — I could count to a hundred at five!

📕 Чтение

School in the past

A hundred years ago, school was very different. Children wrote with chalk on small boards. There weren't any computers, and classes were often very big.

Students had to be very quiet, and teachers were very strict. Many children couldn't stay at school for long because they had to work. Today school is easier and more fun.
