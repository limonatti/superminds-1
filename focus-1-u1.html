A normal weekday

Послушай диалог (озвучивается прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы. Слушай сколько угодно раз.

Lena: Hi Max! What time do you usually get up on school days?
Max: At half past six. I have breakfast and then I go to school by bus.
Lena: Do you have breakfast with your family?
Max: Yes, with my mum and my little sister. My dad starts work early.
Lena: What do you do after school?
Max: I do my homework, and twice a week I play football with my friends.
Lena: Nice! Do you watch TV in the evening?
Max: Sometimes, but I usually go to bed at ten. I need my sleep!

📕 Чтение

Two families, two homes

Nadia lives in a small flat with her mum, her dad and her baby brother. Every morning her mum makes breakfast and they eat together before school. At the weekend the family often goes to the park.

Ben lives in a house with his grandparents. His parents work in another city, so he only sees them once a month. Ben gets on really well with his grandad — they play chess every evening.

💬 How to… рассказать о себе

I live in… with my… — где и с кем живёшь
I usually get up at… / I go to school by… — распорядок
In my free time I… — хобби
I get on well with… — с кем ладишь
