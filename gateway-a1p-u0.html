🎧 Аудирование

The first day

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Teacher: Good morning! Welcome to your new class. What's your name?
Adam: Good morning. My name's Adam.
Teacher: Nice to meet you, Adam. How do you spell that?
Adam: A-D-A-M.
Teacher: Perfect. Adam, what's this in English?
Adam: It's a dictionary. And that's a computer.
Teacher: Very good! What colour is your bag?
Adam: My bag is blue and my pen is red.
Teacher: Great. Now open your book at page ten, please.
Adam: Sorry, can you repeat that?
Teacher: Of course. Open your book at page ten.

🎧 Аудирование 2

What's the time?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Tom: Emma, what day is it today?
Emma: It's Monday. Why?
Tom: We've got English on Monday. What time is it now?
Emma: It's quarter past eight. English is at nine o'clock.
Tom: Oh good, we aren't late. What time is lunch?
Emma: Lunch is at half past twelve.
Tom: And what's the date today?
Emma: It's the fifth of January.

📕 Чтение

My classroom

This is my English classroom. It is big and bright. There is a green board on the wall and there are twelve desks for the students.

On my desk I have got a book, a blue pen, a pencil and a small ruler. My English dictionary is red and it is very big! My bag is under the chair.

There is a computer near the window and there are colourful posters on the walls. I like my classroom because it is friendly and full of colours.

📕 Чтение 2

A week at school

My name is Nadia and I love school. My favourite day is Monday because we have art in the morning.

Lessons start at nine o'clock and finish at half past three. We have lunch at half past twelve. On Wednesday we finish early, at one o'clock.

The best day is Saturday because there is no school! On Saturday I get up at ten o'clock and I meet my friends. But on Sunday evening I always prepare my bag for Monday morning.

✍️ Writing

My classroom. Опиши свой класс (5–6 предложений): что там есть (There is / There are), какого цвета вещи, и напиши сегодняшний день и число.

Проверь: цвета, числа словами, заглавные буквы у дней и месяцев, времена (It's… o'clock).

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

School around the world

Not all schools start and finish at the same time. Around the world, the school day is different for every student.

In Spain, many students start at nine o'clock and have a long lunch break in the middle of the day. In Japan, students often clean their own classroom after lessons. In Finland, children start school at seven years old — later than in many other countries.

But some things are the same everywhere. Students have got books, pens and pencils, they learn together with friends, and they all look forward to Saturday!
