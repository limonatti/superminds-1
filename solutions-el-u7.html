🎧 Аудирование

A computer help desk

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Helper: Hello, IT help desk. Can I help you?
Caller: Hi. My laptop didn't start this morning.
Helper: OK. Did you charge it last night?
Caller: Yes, I did. I plugged in the charger and left it all night.
Helper: Did you see any message on the screen?
Caller: No, I didn't. The screen stayed black.
Helper: I see. Did you try a different charger?
Caller: No, I didn't think of that. I'll try now… Oh! It works! Thank you!
Helper: Great. The problem was the charger, not the laptop. No problem!

🎧 Аудирование 2

In a shop

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Assistant: Hi, can I help you?
Customer: Yes, I'm looking for some headphones.
Assistant: We have these. They're wireless and connect to your phone.
Customer: Nice. How much are they?
Assistant: They're twenty-five pounds.
Customer: OK. Do they come with a charger?
Assistant: Yes, and a memory stick as a free gift this week.
Customer: Great, I'll take them. Thank you!

📕 Чтение

Dancing Man

A few years ago, someone took a photo of a man dancing alone at a music festival. Other people were laughing at him, and the photo appeared on the internet.

When a group of people saw the photo online, they felt sorry for the man. But instead of laughing, they decided to do something kind. They found the man and invited him to a big party, where hundreds of people danced with him.

The story became famous all over the world. It showed how the internet can be cruel, but also how it can bring people together in a positive way. Sometimes one small action online can change everything.

📕 Чтение 2

My first computer

My grandmother told me about her first computer. She bought it in 1995, and it was very different from computers today.

'It was huge and slow,' she said. 'There was no internet at home, so I couldn't send emails or watch videos. The screen showed only green letters, and I had to save my work on a floppy disk. It made a strange noise every time I turned it on!'

Now my grandmother has a small tablet and she sends me messages every day. 'Technology changed so fast,' she says. 'I never imagined I could carry a computer in my bag.'

✍️ Writing

A narrative. Напиши короткий рассказ (5–6 предложений) о том, как что-то случилось с техникой (например, сломался телефон или потерялся файл): что произошло, что ты сделал и чем всё закончилось.

Проверь: Past simple (правильные и неправильные глаголы), связки времени (first, then, after that, finally).

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Growing up online

Today's teenagers are the first generation to grow up completely online. They send messages, watch videos and play games on their phones every day. But is this a good thing?

On the positive side, the internet gives young people information, new friends and creative tools. A teenager can learn a language, start a small business or share their art with the whole world.

However, there are also problems. Spending too much time online can affect sleep and schoolwork, and not everything on the internet is true. Experts say the answer is balance: use technology to learn and connect, but also spend time offline with real friends and nature.
