🎧 Аудирование

My new flat

Послушай диалог (озвучка прямо в браузере), потом ответь на вопросы.

Nadia: This is my new flat! Come in.
Tom: Wow, it's nice! How many rooms are there?
Nadia: There are four rooms: a living room, a kitchen and two bedrooms. And there's a bathroom, of course.
Tom: Is there a garden?
Nadia: No, there isn't. But there's a balcony next to the living room.
Tom: What's in your room?
Nadia: There's a bed, a wardrobe and a desk. And there are lots of shelves for my books.
Tom: Is there a TV in the living room?
Nadia: Yes, there is. It's on the wall, in front of the sofa.

🎧 Аудирование 2

What's in the fridge?

Послушай второй диалог и ответь на вопросы.

Leo: Mum, I'm hungry! What's in the fridge?
Mum: There's some cheese and there are some eggs. And there's a little chicken.
Leo: Is there any milk? I want a hot chocolate.
Mum: Yes, there's some milk. But there isn't any bread, sorry.
Leo: That's OK. Are there any tomatoes for a salad?
Mum: Yes, there are some tomatoes and some vegetables in the cupboard.
Leo: Great. I can make a cheese and tomato salad!
Mum: Good idea. And drink some water, not too much juice!

📕 Чтение

An amazing tree house

Most people live in a normal house or flat, but the Miller family live in a tree house! It is high up in a big old tree in their garden.

There are three rooms in the tree house. There's a small kitchen with a fridge and a sink, a living room with a sofa, and a bedroom for the two children. There isn't a bathroom in the tree, so the family use the bathroom in their normal house.

'It's fantastic,' says young Emma Miller. 'From my bed I can see the birds and the stars. There isn't a TV, but there are lots of books on the shelves. I love our tree house!'

📕 Чтение 2

A healthy plate

What is a healthy meal? Doctors say a good plate of food has got three important parts.

First, there are vegetables and fruit — for example a salad, some tomatoes or an apple. Second, there is some rice, bread or pasta to give you energy. Third, there is a little meat, fish, cheese or eggs.

It is also important to drink water. Doctors say we shouldn't have too much sugar, so it is better to eat fruit than cake or biscuits. A healthy meal isn't boring — it can be colourful and delicious!

🎯 IELTS-style Exam Trainer (формат экзамена)

Tiny houses: small is beautiful

Some people think a good home must be big. But around the world, more and more people choose to live in tiny houses.

A tiny house is very small — sometimes there is only one room. But there is everything you need: a small kitchen, a bed, a table and a bathroom. Some tiny houses are on wheels, so you can move your home to a new place!

Why do people choose tiny houses? They aren't expensive, they are good for the environment, and there isn't a lot to clean. 'I don't need a big house,' says Lena, who lives in a tiny house near the forest. 'I've got everything here, and I love it.'
